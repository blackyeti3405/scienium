## Physics & Astronomy — subtype emphasis

Beyond the base science rubric, hold a physics/astronomy plan to these specifics:

- **Model & prediction:** Is there an underlying physical model that makes a
  **quantitative, testable prediction** (an equation or scaling relationship)?
- **Variables:** Independent/dependent variables and what is held constant;
  measurement ranges chosen to actually distinguish the predicted effect.
- **Apparatus & precision:** Instrumentation and its resolution/uncertainty;
  sources of systematic error identified.
- **Data & analysis:** Enough data points across the range; planned fitting/
  comparison to theory with error bars and uncertainty propagation.
- **Reproducibility:** Repeated measurements; controls for confounding effects.
