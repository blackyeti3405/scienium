## Biology & Life Sciences — subtype emphasis

Beyond the base science rubric, hold a biology plan to these specifics:

- **System & model:** Is the organism/model/system appropriate and justified for
  the question? Ethical/safety rules (vertebrate, human, BSL) acknowledged?
- **Variables & controls:** Independent/dependent variables and **positive and
  negative controls** clearly defined. Confounds (age, environment, batch) named.
- **Replication & power:** Adequate **sample size** and biological replicates;
  randomization/blinding where relevant.
- **Measurement:** Outcome measures are objective and quantifiable; assays
  available to the team.
- **Analysis:** Appropriate statistics planned (which test, why); reproducible.
