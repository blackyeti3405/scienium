## Materials & Chemical Engineering — subtype emphasis

Beyond the base engineering rubric, hold a materials/chemical-eng plan to these:

- **Target properties:** Are the properties to optimize defined and **measurable**
  (strength, conductivity, degradation rate, yield, purity)?
- **Process variables:** Are the synthesis/process parameters (concentration,
  temperature, time, ratios) identified as the variables to control and vary?
- **Characterization:** What methods measure the outcomes (mechanical tests,
  spectroscopy, microscopy, titration)? Are they available to the team?
- **Controls & replicates:** Baseline/control samples and **repeated batches** to
  separate real effects from variability.
- **Safety:** Hazards, handling, and disposal addressed; ISEF rules considered.
