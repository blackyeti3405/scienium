## Chemistry — subtype emphasis

Beyond the base science rubric, hold a chemistry plan to these specifics:

- **System:** Are the reactions/analytes and the property being measured clearly
  defined? Is the chemistry sound and feasible at a school scale?
- **Variables & controls:** Concentration, temperature, pH, catalyst, time as
  controlled/varied factors; blanks and standards as controls.
- **Measurement:** Quantitative methods (titration, spectrophotometry,
  chromatography, gravimetry) with attention to precision and calibration.
- **Replicates & error:** Repeated trials, error/uncertainty treatment, and
  appropriate significant figures.
- **Safety:** Hazards, PPE, handling, and disposal addressed; ISEF rules met.
