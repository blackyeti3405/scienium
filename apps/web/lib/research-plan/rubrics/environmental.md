## Environmental Science — subtype emphasis

Beyond the base science rubric, hold an environmental plan to these specifics:

- **Question & scope:** Is the environmental question focused and the system
  (site, organism, pollutant, parameter) well-defined?
- **Sampling design:** Spatial/temporal **sampling plan** justified — sites,
  replicates, timing — to capture variability and avoid pseudo-replication.
- **Variables & controls:** Treatments vs. controls/reference sites; confounds
  (weather, season, location) identified.
- **Measurement:** Field/lab methods with adequate precision; calibration of
  probes/kits.
- **Analysis:** Appropriate statistics for environmental/field data; honest
  treatment of variability and limitations.
