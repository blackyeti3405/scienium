## Mechanical & Structural — subtype emphasis

Beyond the base engineering rubric, hold a mechanical/structural plan to these:

- **Loads & requirements:** Are the forces, stresses, or flow conditions the
  design must handle quantified? Are performance targets measurable?
- **Analysis:** Is there back-of-envelope or simulation analysis (statics,
  stress, factor of safety, fluid/thermal) justifying the design before building?
- **Materials & fabrication:** Material choices justified for strength/weight/
  cost; fabrication method realistic for the team's tools.
- **Alternatives:** Competing mechanisms/geometries compared, not one assumed.
- **Testing:** Instrumented tests (force, displacement, durability cycles) with
  quantitative metrics across **repeated trials** and failure analysis.
