## Electrical & Embedded — subtype emphasis

Beyond the base engineering rubric, hold an electrical/embedded plan to these:

- **Circuit design:** Are key components specified with ratings (voltage,
  current, power, tolerance)? Is there a schematic or block diagram plan?
- **Sensing & signals:** Sensor selection justified for range/resolution/noise?
  Is signal conditioning (amplification, filtering, ADC resolution) addressed?
- **Power:** Power budget, regulation, and (if battery) a **runtime** estimate.
- **Measurement plan:** What instruments verify behavior (multimeter, scope,
  logic analyzer)? Quantitative metrics over repeated trials and conditions.
- **Safety & robustness:** Thermal, isolation, and failure modes considered.
