## Behavioral & Social Sciences — subtype emphasis

Beyond the base science rubric, hold a behavioral/social plan to these specifics:

- **Hypothesis & constructs:** Are the constructs operationally defined and the
  hypothesis testable? Is the design correlational or experimental (and is the
  causal language matched to it)?
- **Participants & ethics:** Sampling, recruitment, **sample size**, and consent;
  ISEF human-participant rules (Form 4 / IRB) acknowledged.
- **Measures:** Validated instruments or clearly-defined coded measures;
  reliability considered; randomization/counterbalancing/blinding where relevant.
- **Bias & confounds:** Response bias, order effects, and confounds identified
  and controlled.
- **Analysis:** Appropriate statistics planned; sample size adequate to detect
  the effect; reproducible coding scheme.
