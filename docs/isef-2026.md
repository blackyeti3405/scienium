# ISEF 2026 — Project Board & Judging Criteria (reference)

Source: Regeneron ISEF "What to Expect" (provided by Rig, 2026-06-18). Used by the
Science Fair gateway (Feature 3) for the ISEF rubric view, and as the basis for the
engineering/science review criteria in the Research Plan building (Feature 2B).

## Project board guidance
- **Graphic credits & citations:** every graphic/chart created by the finalist must be
  individually cited ("Photo taken by Finalist", "Graph created by Finalist using …").
  Graphics not created by the finalist must be cited individually (APA preferred); if
  obtained online, include a URL/DOI — even when the license doesn't require it.
  Citations go alongside the graphic or in a vertical reference list.
- Important information should be readable from ~10 feet away; smallest poster font ~18 pt.
- Title is short and draws interest.
- Word count ~300–800 words; text clear and to the point.
- Use bullets, numbering, headlines; effective use of graphics, color, fonts.
- Bring special forms (1C, Form 4/Informed Consent, Continuation Form, etc.) and photo ID for setup.

## Judging overview
Science and engineering use **different criteria**, each with **five sections** and suggested
scoring. Considerable emphasis on **Creativity & Potential Impact** and **Presentation/Interview**.
- **Creativity & Potential Impact:** imagination/inventiveness; new perspectives/alternatives;
  real or potential impact on the world (judges weight research outcomes & analysis).
- **Presentation/Interview:** evaluates understanding of the project's basic science,
  interpretation, and limitations. Independence (Form 1C), mentoring, and — for multi-year
  work — ONLY the current year's progress (Form 7). Team and individual projects are judged
  together on quality; all team members must show significant contribution and understanding.

## Judging Criteria — Science Projects
- **I. Research Question (10 pts):** clear and focused purpose; identifies contribution to
  field of study; testable using scientific methods.
- **II. Design and Methodology (15 pts):** well-designed plan and data-collection methods;
  variables and controls defined, appropriate, and complete.
- **III. Execution: Data Collection, Analysis and Interpretation (20 pts):** systematic data
  collection and analysis; reproducibility; appropriate math/statistical methods; sufficient
  data to support interpretation and conclusions.
- **IV. Creativity & Potential Impact (20 pts):** significant creativity in one or more of the
  above; impact or potential impact in field/technology/economy/environment/society.
- **V. Presentation (35 pts):**
  - a. Poster (10 pts): logical organization; clarity of graphics/legends; supporting docs displayed.
  - b. Interview (25 pts): clear/concise/thoughtful responses; understanding of basic science;
    interpretation and limitations of results; degree of independence; recognition of potential
    impact; quality of ideas for further research; for teams, contribution & understanding by all members.

## Judging Criteria — Engineering Projects
- **I. Research Problem (10 pts):** description of a practical need/problem; definition of criteria
  for the proposed solution; explanation of constraints.
- **II. Design and Methodology (15 pts):** exploration of alternatives; identification of a solution;
  development of a prototype/model.
- **III. Execution: Construction and Testing (20 pts):** prototype demonstrates intended design;
  tested in multiple conditions/trials; demonstrates engineering skill and completeness.
- **IV. Creativity & Potential Impact (20 pts):** significant creativity in one or more of the above;
  impact or potential impact in field/technology/economy/environment/society.
- **V. Presentation (35 pts):**
  - a. Poster (10 pts): logical organization; clarity of graphics/legends; supporting docs displayed.
  - b. Interview (25 pts): clear/concise/thoughtful responses; understanding of basic science;
    interpretation and limitations of results; degree of independence; recognition of potential
    impact; quality of ideas for further research; for teams, contribution & understanding by all members.
