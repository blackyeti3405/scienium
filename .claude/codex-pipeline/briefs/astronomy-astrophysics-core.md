## Unit plan (fundamentals → light → instruments → measurement → interpretation → landmark → applications)

1. **The Sky and Its Objects** — celestial coordinates (RA/Dec); apparent vs absolute magnitude; flux/luminosity/inverse-square; the EM spectrum across astronomy; the census of objects; angular size & parallax.
2. **Light as Data** — blackbody radiation & Wien's law; Stefan–Boltzmann & luminosity; emission/absorption & Kirchhoff's laws; spectral lines & composition; Doppler & radial velocity; redshift & cosmic expansion.
3. **Telescopes and Detectors** — refractors/reflectors & light-gathering; angular resolution & the diffraction limit; seeing & adaptive optics; CCDs & quantum efficiency; observing radio→X-ray; telescopes in space.
4. **Photometry and Spectroscopy** — aperture photometry; photometric calibration & standard stars; color indices; building/reading a spectrum; spectral classification (OBAFGKM); raw frames → calibrated data (bias/dark/flat).
5. **Reading the Diagnostic Plots** — the HR diagram; color–magnitude diagrams & dating clusters; light curves (variables/eclipses/transits); radial-velocity curves; galaxy rotation curves; period–luminosity & standard candles.
6. **From Signal to Claim** — the cosmic distance ladder; parallax & the parsec; standard candles (Cepheids, Type Ia SNe); error bars/S:N/significance; systematic vs random error; reasoning from photons to conclusions.
7. **Landmark Work & Open Debates** *(required)* — the Great Debate (Shapley–Curtis 1920); Leavitt's period–luminosity law; Hubble & the expanding universe; dark matter (Zwicky, Rubin rotation curves); the accelerating universe & dark energy (1998, Nobel 2011); the Hubble tension.
8. **Applications & Designing an Astronomy Study** — mining archives (SDSS, Gaia, TESS); real photometry & AAVSO variable stars; exoplanet transits from public data; image stacking/processing; radio & citizen science; designing a defensible science-fair astronomy project (usually data-driven).
