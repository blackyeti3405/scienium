## Scope (TAXONOMY)
Hormone-and-metabolism drugs and tests: insulin and the glucose tolerance curve, diabetes/
thyroid agents, hormone feedback loops, and metabolic response to a challenge. Clinical-drug
framing — distinct from a food-composition or diet-design angle (that's nutrition-food-science).

## Unit plan — 7 units, ~45 lessons
1. `clinpharm-endo-foundations` **Endocrine Foundations for Pharmacology** (6) — hormones as chemical signals; endocrine vs exocrine; membrane (fast, GPCR) vs nuclear (slow, gene) receptors; negative-feedback loops and set points; the axis concept (hypothalamus→pituitary→gland); agonist/antagonist/replacement at hormone receptors.
2. `clinpharm-endo-glucose` **Glucose Homeostasis & the Insulin System** (7) — insulin vs glucagon; how blood glucose is regulated; insulin signaling and GLUT transporters; the oral glucose tolerance test curve; HbA1c as a time-averaged marker; incretins (GLP-1/GIP) and the incretin effect.
3. `clinpharm-endo-diabetes` **Diabetes Pharmacotherapy** (7) — type 1 vs type 2 pathophysiology; insulin preparations and their action-time curves (rapid/short/intermediate/long); metformin; sulfonylureas; GLP-1 receptor agonists; SGLT2 inhibitors; DPP-4 inhibitors — each mechanism → measurable glucose effect.
4. `clinpharm-endo-thyroid` **Thyroid, Adrenal & Other Axes** (6) — the thyroid axis (TRH/TSH/T4/T3, feedback); levothyroxine replacement and antithyroid drugs (methimazole/PTU); the adrenal axis and glucocorticoids (cortisol, dexamethasone, feedback suppression); a brief look at sex-hormone pharmacology.
5. `clinpharm-endo-tests` **Endocrine Tests & Dynamic Challenges** (6) — static hormone levels vs DYNAMIC tests; the OGTT protocol and curve; suppression tests (dexamethasone) vs stimulation tests; reading a challenge curve (baseline→response→recovery); reference ranges, timing, and assay pitfalls.
6. `clinpharm-endo-landmark` **Landmark Work & Open Debates** (7) **[landmark]** — Banting & Best and the discovery of insulin (1921-22; 1923 Nobel to Banting & Macleod); the DCCT/UKPDS trials establishing glycemic control matters; the ACCORD trial and the intensive-glucose-control controversy (harm signal); the CV-outcome-trial revolution for GLP-1/SGLT2 agents; the UGDP sulfonylurea controversy; HbA1c standardization. VERIFY names/dates with WebSearch.
7. `clinpharm-endo-apply` **Applications & Safe Study Design** (6) — non-invasive/minimally-invasive metabolic studies (glycemic response to different foods/drinks via finger-stick or CGM — with strict ethics), the meal-challenge design, confounders (fasting state, exercise, time of day), honest analysis of a glucose curve, the bright line (students never dose insulin/hormones), a worked glycemic-index mini-project.

## Depth requirements
Landmark people/dates (Banting & Best 1921-22, 1923 Nobel; DCCT; ACCORD); debates (tight-control
harm in ACCORD, UGDP, CV-outcome trials reshaping first-line choice); competing methods (static
levels vs dynamic challenge; HbA1c vs fasting glucose vs OGTT). Judge framing specific per lesson.
