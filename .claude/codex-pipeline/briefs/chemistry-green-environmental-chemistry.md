# Brief — chemistry · green-environmental-chemistry (branch)

- **field:** `chemistry` · **branch id/label:** `green-environmental-chemistry` / Green & Environmental Chemistry
- **target:** 7 units × 6 lessons = **42 lessons** · **idPrefix:** `chemistry-green`
- **scope:** reaction efficiency, atom economy, safer solvents and catalysts, water/soil contaminant chemistry, and remediation — the chemistry of reducing waste and hazards, framed by reactions and analysis.

## Unit plan
1. **Principles of Green Chemistry** (`principles`) — the 12 principles (Anastas & Warner); prevention over cleanup; benign by design; the E-factor idea.
2. **Atom Economy and Reaction Efficiency** (`atomeconomy`) — atom economy vs percent yield; the E-factor; process mass intensity; comparing routes.
3. **Safer Solvents, Reagents, and Catalysis** (`safersolvents`) — solvent hazards; water/scCO2/ionic liquids; catalytic vs stoichiometric; biocatalysis.
4. **Renewable Feedstocks and Energy Efficiency** (`feedstocks`) — bio-based vs petroleum feedstocks; energy efficiency; designing for degradation; life-cycle thinking.
5. **Water and Soil Contaminant Chemistry** (`contaminants`) — heavy metals, nitrates, organics; speciation; transport; measuring contaminants.
6. **Landmark Work & Open Debates** (`landmark`, required) — Carson's Silent Spring & DDT; the 12 principles (1998); Montreal Protocol & ozone (Molina/Rowland/Crutzen, Nobel 1995); Trost's atom economy (1991); the greenwashing debate; PFAS.
7. **Remediation and Green Analysis** (`remediation`) — adsorption, bioremediation, advanced oxidation; green analytical chemistry; measuring cleanup; a defensible remediation claim.

**Total: 7 × 6 = 42 lessons.**
