## Unit plan (fundamentals → methods → interpretation → landmark/debates → advanced → applications)

1. **What Sound Is** — pressure wave; frequency/wavelength/period/amplitude; speed of sound & what changes it; longitudinal vs transverse (sound vs light); wavefronts/rays; SHM & the pure tone.
2. **Loudness, Level, and the Decibel** — pressure vs power vs intensity; why logarithmic; dB SPL & the 20 µPa reference; adding levels (+3 dB); A/C weighting; dynamic range, threshold of hearing to pain.
3. **Frequency, Spectra, and Timbre** — waveform vs spectrum; fundamental & harmonics; Fourier's idea; the FFT & reading a spectrum; pitch vs frequency & the missing fundamental; timbre as spectral shape + envelope.
4. **Propagation, Reflection, and Resonance** — inverse-square spreading; reflection/absorption/transmission/diffraction; standing waves in strings & air columns; resonance, natural frequencies & Q; interference & beats; the Doppler effect.
5. **Measuring Sound: Instruments and Method** — microphone types & directionality; sound level meter & calibration; sampling rate & the Nyquist limit; aliasing & anti-aliasing; background noise & the signal chain; reading time/spectrum/spectrogram plots.
6. **Landmark Work & Open Debates** *(required)* — Pythagoras & the monochord (consonance); Helmholtz resonance & place theory of hearing; Fletcher–Munson equal-loudness contours & revisions; Békésy's traveling wave vs rivals (Nobel 1961); perceptual coding & the MP3; the "loudness war" dynamic-range-compression debate.
7. **Advanced: Waves, Nonlinearity & Digital Audio** — acoustic impedance & transmission between media; the wave equation intuitively; quantization/bit depth/noise floor; the STFT & spectrograms; nonlinear acoustics (shock/distortion/cavitation); room modes & modal density.
8. **Applications & Designing an Acoustics Study** — environmental noise measurement & regulation; room acoustics RT60 & speech intelligibility; analyzing instruments & the voice; ultrasound & sonar; bioacoustics; designing a defensible science-fair acoustics experiment.
