# Brief — chemistry · organic-synthesis (branch)

- **field:** `chemistry` · **branch id/label:** `organic-synthesis` / Organic Chemistry & Synthesis
- **target:** 7 units × 6 lessons = **42 lessons** · **idPrefix:** `chemistry-organic`
- **scope:** carbon structure, functional groups, reaction mechanisms, stereochemistry; designing/running/purifying a synthesis (extraction, recrystallization, distillation) and confirming product identity + yield.

## Unit plan
1. **Structure and Bonding in Organic Molecules** (`structure`) — hybridization; sigma/pi; skeletal/condensed structures; resonance; formal charge; degrees of unsaturation.
2. **Functional Groups and Nomenclature** (`functionalgroups`) — the major functional groups; IUPAC naming; identifying groups; reactivity by group.
3. **Isomerism and Stereochemistry** (`stereochemistry`) — constitutional isomers; conformations (Newman); chirality; R/S; enantiomers/diastereomers; optical activity; meso.
4. **Reaction Mechanisms** (`mechanisms`) — curved arrows; nucleophiles/electrophiles; SN1/SN2; E1/E2; electrophilic addition; reactive intermediates.
5. **Key Reactions and Transformations** (`reactions`) — substitution/elimination/addition; oxidation/reduction; carbonyl nucleophilic addition; the interconversion map; multi-step logic.
6. **Landmark Work & Open Debates** (`landmark`, required) — Wöhler urea (vitalism); Kekulé benzene; Van't Hoff/Le Bel tetrahedral carbon; Woodward total synthesis; Woodward–Hoffmann rules; Corey retrosynthesis (Nobel 1990).
7. **Running and Confirming a Synthesis** (`labwork`) — planning; liquid-liquid extraction; recrystallization; distillation; TLC monitoring; confirming identity (mp/IR/NMR) and yield.

**Total: 7 × 6 = 42 lessons.**
