# Brief — chemistry · analytical-chemistry (branch)

- **field:** `chemistry` · **branch id/label:** `analytical-chemistry` / Analytical Chemistry
- **target:** 7 units × 6 lessons = **42 lessons** (SUBFIELD_LESSONS 40–50) · **idPrefix:** `chemistry-analytical`
- **scope (TAXONOMY):** quantitative/qualitative identification — spectroscopy (UV-Vis, IR, atomic absorption), chromatography (HPLC, GC, TLC), titrimetry, electrochemical sensors; calibration curves + validating detection limits for a real unknown.

## Unit plan
1. **Foundations of Quantitative Analysis** (`foundations`) — the analytical question; accuracy/precision/error; calibration strategies; signal/noise/LOD/LOQ; sampling & prep; analytical statistics.
2. **Titrimetric Analysis** (`titrimetry`) — acid-base curves; EDTA complexometric; redox; precipitation; primary standards; titration uncertainty.
3. **Molecular Spectroscopy: UV-Vis and IR** (`spectroscopy`) — Beer-Lambert; UV-Vis; deviations; IR/FTIR; fluorescence; reading spectra.
4. **Atomic Spectroscopy** (`atomic`) — flame AA; graphite furnace; ICP-OES; interferences; atomic vs molecular; trace-metal case.
5. **Chromatography: Separations** (`chromatography`) — separation principle; TLC; GC; HPLC; efficiency/resolution (van Deemter); detectors & hyphenated MS.
6. **Landmark Work & Open Debates** (`landmark`, required) — Tswett; Beer/Lambert/Bouguer; Martin & Synge (Nobel 1952); the detection-limit debate; rise of GC-MS/LC-MS; method validation & reproducibility.
7. **Method Validation and Defending an Analysis** (`validation`) — calibration curve/linearity; LOD/LOQ; recovery/spikes; precision; selectivity; reporting an unknown with uncertainty.

**Total: 7 × 6 = 42 lessons.**
