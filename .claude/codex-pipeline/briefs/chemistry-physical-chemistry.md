# Brief — chemistry · physical-chemistry (branch)

- **field:** `chemistry` · **branch id/label:** `physical-chemistry` / Physical Chemistry
- **target:** 7 units × 6 lessons = **42 lessons** · **idPrefix:** `chemistry-physchem`
- **scope:** the quantitative theory beneath reactions — kinetics and rate-law determination, thermodynamics, phase equilibria and phase diagrams, colligative properties, and the physical models (collision theory, transition state) that explain rate and spontaneity. Goes DEEPER/more quantitative than Core's thermo/kinetics.

## Unit plan
1. **The Gas Phase and Kinetic Molecular Theory** (`gases`) — ideal gas law; KMT; the Maxwell–Boltzmann speed distribution; real gases (van der Waals); effusion (Graham).
2. **Chemical Thermodynamics (Quantitative)** (`thermo`) — first law; enthalpy; entropy & the second law; Gibbs free energy; the third law; standard states.
3. **Phase Equilibria and Phase Diagrams** (`phase`) — P–T phase diagrams; Clausius–Clapeyron; triple & critical points; the Gibbs phase rule; transitions.
4. **Solutions and Colligative Properties** (`solutions`) — Raoult's law; ideal vs real solutions; BP elevation / FP depression / osmotic pressure; the van't Hoff factor; activity.
5. **Chemical Kinetics and Rate Laws** (`kinetics`) — rate laws; determining order; integrated rate laws; Arrhenius; mechanisms; steady-state approximation.
6. **Landmark Work & Open Debates** (`landmark`, required) — Boltzmann & statistical mechanics (S = k ln W); Gibbs & chemical thermodynamics; Arrhenius (Nobel 1903); Eyring transition-state theory; Onsager reciprocal relations (Nobel 1968); Marcus electron-transfer theory (Nobel 1992).
7. **Physical Models: Collision, Transition State, Statistical Thermo** (`models`) — collision theory; transition-state (activated-complex) theory; the Boltzmann factor & partition function (concept); linking micro to macro; catalysis via the barrier.

**Total: 7 × 6 = 42 lessons.** Ships chemistry to ✅ (Core + 5 branches = 258 lessons).
