- **idPrefix:** `cmp-gravitation` (lesson ids → `cmp-gravitation-<unitShort>-<slug>`)
- **scope:** Newtonian gravity as a force law — g at a planet's surface, Kepler's laws, circular and elliptical orbits, and escape velocity. The mechanics/force-law angle on orbits.

## Unit plan (force law → field/g → energy/escape → circular orbits → Kepler → maneuvers/tides → landmark)
1. **Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation** — F = G·m₁m₂/r²; the constant G and the Cavendish torsion-balance measurement; superposition of forces; the shell theorem (a uniform sphere pulls as a point mass at its center). *(6)*
2. **Gravitational Field and Surface Gravity g** — the field g = GM/r²; why g changes with altitude, latitude, and planet; weight vs mass revisited; g *inside* a uniform planet (falls linearly to zero at the center); measuring g. *(6)*
3. **Gravitational Potential Energy and Escape Velocity** — U = −GMm/r and the potential well (why not mgh far from a planet); escape velocity v = √(2GM/R); bound vs unbound trajectories; total orbital energy. *(6)*
4. **Circular Orbits and Weightlessness** — orbital speed v = √(GM/r) and period; "falling around" the planet; apparent weightlessness as free fall (not absence of gravity); geostationary orbit; satellites and GPS. *(6)*
5. **Kepler's Laws and Elliptical Orbits** — ellipses, foci, semi-major axis; the equal-area law as conservation of angular momentum; the harmonic law T² ∝ a³ (derived for a circle); perihelion vs aphelion speed; deriving Kepler from Newton. *(7)*
6. **Orbital Maneuvers, Tides, and Many-Body Gravity** — the Hohmann transfer (qualitative energy picture); the gravitational slingshot/assist; Lagrange points; tides as differential gravity; the leap from the solvable two-body problem to the chaotic n-body problem; spacecraft-trajectory applications. *(7)*
7. **Landmark Work & Open Debates** *(required)* — Kepler distilling his laws from Tycho Brahe's data; Newton's synthesis and the "action at a distance" objection; the 1846 discovery of Neptune by predicted perturbation (Le Verrier/Adams); Mercury's perihelion precession as the crack where Newtonian gravity failed (→ general relativity); dark matter inferred from galactic dynamics (the force-law reading). *(6)*

**Total: 6+6+6+6+7+7+6 = 44 lessons.**
