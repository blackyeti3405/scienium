# Brief — chemistry · electrochemistry (branch)

- **field:** `chemistry` · **branch id/label:** `electrochemistry` / Electrochemistry
- **target:** 7 units × 6 lessons = **42 lessons** · **idPrefix:** `chemistry-electro`
- **scope:** redox, half-cells, cell potentials and the Nernst equation, electrolysis, corrosion — quantifying electron transfer in batteries, sensors, and plating.

## Unit plan
1. **Redox Foundations for Electrochemistry** (`redoxfoundations`) — oxidation states; half-reactions; balancing redox (acidic/basic); oxidizing/reducing agents; the electron-transfer picture.
2. **Galvanic Cells and Cell Potential** (`galvanic`) — anode/cathode; salt bridge; cell notation; standard reduction potentials (SHE); E°cell.
3. **Thermodynamics of Electrochemistry** (`thermo`) — ΔG = −nFE; spontaneity from E°; K from E°; electrical work.
4. **The Nernst Equation and Non-Standard Cells** (`nernst`) — Nernst equation; concentration dependence; concentration cells; pH/ion-selective electrodes.
5. **Electrolysis and Applied Electrochemistry** (`electrolysis`) — electrolytic cells; Faraday's laws; electroplating; electrorefining; the chlor-alkali process.
6. **Landmark Work & Open Debates** (`landmark`, required) — Galvani vs Volta; Faraday's laws & terms; Nernst (Nobel 1920); the Daniell cell; lithium-ion (Goodenough/Whittingham/Yoshino, Nobel 2019); hydrogen/fuel-cell debate.
7. **Corrosion, Batteries, and Sensors** (`applications`) — corrosion & cathodic protection; battery chemistries (primary/secondary, Li-ion); fuel cells; electrochemical sensors (glucose, pH, ISE).

**Total: 7 × 6 = 42 lessons.**
