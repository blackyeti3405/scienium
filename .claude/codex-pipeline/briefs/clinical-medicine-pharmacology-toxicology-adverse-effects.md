## Scope (TAXONOMY)
The dark side of the dose-response curve: LD50 and dose-toxicity, overdose and the therapeutic
index, drug interactions, poisons and antidotes, and adverse-reaction and safety-signal analysis.

## Unit plan — 7 units, ~45 lessons
1. `clinpharm-tox-foundations` **The Dose Makes the Poison** (6) — Paracelsus' principle; the toxic dose-response curve; LD50/TD50 and how they're defined; therapeutic index (TD50/ED50) and margin of safety; acute vs chronic toxicity; hormesis and the J-shaped curve as a controversy teaser.
2. `clinpharm-tox-mechanisms` **Mechanisms of Toxicity** (6) — on-target (exaggerated pharmacology) vs off-target toxicity; reactive metabolites and oxidative stress; organ-specific toxicity (hepatotoxicity, nephrotoxicity, cardiotoxicity, neurotoxicity); the acetaminophen→NAPQI bioactivation story as the model mechanism.
3. `clinpharm-tox-overdose` **Overdose & the Therapeutic Window** (6) — how overdose crosses the window; dose-toxicity of specific agents (acetaminophen, opioids/respiratory depression, digoxin, iron); QT prolongation and torsades as a drug-safety signal; the toxicokinetics of overdose (saturation).
4. `clinpharm-tox-interactions` **Drug Interactions & Adverse Reactions** (7) — PK interactions (CYP450 induction/inhibition) vs PD interactions (additive/synergistic effect); dangerous combinations (serotonin syndrome, CNS-depressant stacking); ADR classification (Type A dose-related vs Type B idiosyncratic); allergy vs intolerance; black-box warnings.
5. `clinpharm-tox-antidotes` **Poisons, Antidotes & Toxicology Testing** (6) — the antidote concept (competitive antagonist, functional antagonist, chelator, enzyme reactivator); classic pairs (naloxone/opioid, N-acetylcysteine/acetaminophen, atropine + pralidoxime/organophosphate, flumazenil/benzodiazepine, digoxin-Fab); decontamination (activated charcoal); how LD50/toxicity is tested.
6. `clinpharm-tox-landmark` **Landmark Work & Open Debates** (7) **[landmark]** — the 1937 sulfanilamide-elixir (diethylene glycol) tragedy → the 1938 FD&C Act; the thalidomide disaster (1957-62) → Kelsey and modern drug regulation; the acetaminophen/NAC antidote discovery; the LD50/Draize animal-test controversy and the 3Rs (replacement/reduction/refinement); the Vioxx (rofecoxib) 2004 withdrawal and pharmacovigilance/signal detection; the Ames test for mutagenicity. VERIFY names/dates with WebSearch.
7. `clinpharm-tox-apply` **Applications & Safe Study Design** (6) — modeling dose-response toxicity with SAFE non-vertebrate surrogates (brine shrimp *Artemia* LC50, seed-germination assay, yeast, *Daphnia*, plant roots); WHY students model toxicity rather than dose people or vertebrates; ethics and IACUC/invertebrate rules; reading and fitting an LC50/dose-response curve (probit/logistic intuition); a fully worked brine-shrimp or seed-germination LC50 mini-project.

## Depth requirements
Landmark events/people/dates (sulfanilamide 1937/FD&C 1938; thalidomide + Frances Kelsey; Vioxx
2004); debates (hormesis, animal LD50 testing/3Rs, over-warning); competing methods (LD50 vs
alternatives; probit vs logistic dose-response fits). Judge framing specific per lesson. Safety
framing is paramount — every applied lesson uses non-vertebrate/safe surrogates only.
