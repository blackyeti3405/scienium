Expands the TAXONOMY 6-unit skeleton to full depth: splits Reactions/Redox from Equilibrium/Acids–Bases and Thermodynamics from Kinetics, and adds the required dedicated **Landmark Work & Open Debates** unit.

## Unit plan (fundamentals → quantitative → reactions → equilibrium → energy → rate → landmark → measurement)

1. **Atoms, Bonding, and the Structure of Matter** (`atoms`) — atom/isotopes; electron configuration & quantum numbers; periodic trends; ionic/covalent/metallic bonding; VSEPR geometry; polarity & intermolecular forces.
2. **The Mole, Stoichiometry, and Solutions** (`mole`) — the mole & Avogadro; balancing & mole ratios; limiting reagent & percent yield; empirical/molecular formulas; molarity & dilution; ideal gas law & gas stoichiometry.
3. **Reactions and Redox** (`react`) — classifying reactions; aqueous & net-ionic equations; oxidation states; balancing redox (half-reactions); activity series; redox in the world (combustion/corrosion/batteries).
4. **Equilibrium, Acids, Bases, and Buffers** (`equil`) — dynamic equilibrium & K; Le Chatelier; acid–base theories & strength; pH/pKa & Kw; buffers & titration curves; solubility equilibria (Ksp).
5. **Thermodynamics: Energy, Entropy, and Spontaneity** (`thermo`) — first law & calorimetry; enthalpy & Hess's law; entropy & the second law; Gibbs free energy & spontaneity; heating/cooling curves; ΔG, Q, and coupling.
6. **Kinetics: Rate, Mechanism, and Catalysis** (`kinetics`) — rate & rate laws; integrated rate laws & half-life; collision theory & activation energy; Arrhenius & temperature; mechanisms & rate-determining step; catalysis.
7. **Landmark Work & Open Debates** (`landmark`, required) — Lavoisier & conservation of mass; Mendeleev's table & its predictions; the acid–base concept's evolution; Avogadro & the 2019 mole redefinition; Haber–Bosch & its costs; green chemistry's 12 principles debate.
8. **Measurement, Analysis, and Defending Results** (`measure`) — sig figs/precision/accuracy; uncertainty & error propagation; calibration curves & Beer–Lambert; UV-Vis & IR/FTIR spectroscopy; chromatography (HPLC/GC/TLC); from data to a defensible claim.

**Total: 8 units × 6 lessons = 48 lessons.**
