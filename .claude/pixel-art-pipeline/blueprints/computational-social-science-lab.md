## Signature feature (one-of-one — a hovering HORIZONTAL society-simulation PLANE swarming with thousands of glowing citizen-dots flocking into clusters across a faint city-grid, social-tie threads rippling, an optimization dial steering toward the best-outcome pattern)
**Above the open oculus floats a HORIZONTAL society-simulation PLANE: a flat translucent sheet swarming with countless tiny glowing citizen-dots that flock into emergent clusters across a faint city-grid, thin social-tie threads rippling between them, while a small optimization DIAL steers the population toward a best-outcome pattern.** Per the collision rule this is explicitly a FLAT horizontal swarm PLANE, NOT a 3D node-graph orb/ring/sphere — distinct from Volt's neural-network ORB, BioCore's genome RING, and ChemWorks' ball-and-stick MOLECULE (those are 3D balls/rings of nodes; this is a top-down floor of thousands of dots flocking on a grid), and the dial is a steering gauge, not TerraBase's branching optimization fork.
- **Society plane:** a flat translucent `c`/`M` sheet hovering level above the oculus (a faint iso parallelogram), under-laid with a faint `d`/`D` city-grid of thin lines — clearly a horizontal plane, not a sphere.
- **Citizen-dots:** a dense scatter of tiny `h`/`c`/`o` glowing dots across the plane, clumping into 2–3 emergent `O`-bright clusters (the flocking) with sparser dots between.
- **Social-tie threads:** faint `o`/`g` magenta threads linking nearby dots, rippling outward from the clusters (kept thin so it never reads as a node-graph ball).
- **Optimization dial:** a small `K`-framed `o`/`O` dial/gauge at the plane's edge with a needle swinging toward a green-lit `c`/`h` best-outcome mark = the optimization (steering the society toward the optimal pattern); a faint `O` glow as the pattern locks in.
