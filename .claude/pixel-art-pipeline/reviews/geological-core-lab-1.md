# Review: Geological Core Lab (terrabase) — round 1

**VERDICT: TWEAK**

The core signature improved a lot — the inclined glass-cased core now shows distinct
ruled strata bands with a teal scanner collar, a glowing scanned band, and a connected
mineral label card (round-0 "no bands / floating" is partly fixed). But two round-0
blockers survived: (a) the BUILT FORM still reads as a tall near-cubic strata block with
a low shed glued to its side — NOT the contracted long low hillside shed; and (b) the
core rack is still free-standing in the void BELOW the mass, separated from the front wall
by the `K` drop-shadow, instead of mounted on a lean-to attached to the building. The
hero LEFT door is also still a dark cluttered opening, not a crisp framed roll-up door.

## Per-criterion

1. **Iso 2:1?** PASS. Diamond top and 2:1 edge slopes are consistent and correct.

2. **Roof visible 3/4 top-down?** PARTIAL/FAIL. A large top face is visible (cols 8–32,
   rows 1–18), but it reads as a flat near-square cube lid carrying the strata texture +
   scattered `r`/`o` specks — there is NO legible high-back / low-front mono-pitch slope.
   The low shed tail to the right (cols 34–48) does have a tilted plane with `d` seam
   lines (good corrugation there), but the dominant left mass is still a flat lid. The
   round-0 note "make the roof a real mono-pitch slope, not a flat lid" is unresolved on
   the main mass.

3. **Front corner faces viewer?** PASS. The near vertical corner (col ~18) points at the
   viewer; both left and right faces read.

4. **Sharp/clean/in-palette/clean K outline/no strays?** PASS. Verified at source res
   (64×64): exactly **13 in-palette colors, zero off-palette, zero anti-alias**, hard `K`
   silhouette, no stray pixels. Technically clean.

5. **Cohesive TerraBase palette + motifs (strata hillside)?** PARTIAL→GOOD on strata.
   The strata cut (left mass, rows 4–28) is genuinely the strongest element: clean
   horizontal-ish bands of `r` rust, `D`/`d` brown, `M` ochre, `L`/`W` pale — real
   stratigraphy and the most strata-forward building in the set. Mineral-teal `g`/`G`/`c`
   glow now appears in the signature. Weakness: the right-hand shed and front wall are
   still an undifferentiated `M`/`D` brown mass; the strata berm does not clearly "butt
   into" the shed back as a carved-in hill — the two masses read as two stacked boxes.

6. **One-of-one signature present + legible + distinct?** PARTIAL (much improved, not
   yet shipping). Inside the `K`-framed glass case (rows 46–48, cols 13–29) there are now
   **distinct ruled bands**: `r` rust → `O`/`h` lit band → `g` teal scanner collar → `D`
   brown → `L` pale → `d` dark. The scanner collar (`g`) and the brightest scanned band
   (`O`/`h`) read, and a `W` label card with a `K` tick + `o` dot (rows 43–45, cols 6–10)
   connects back via a `g`/`G` teal trail. This is a clearly HORIZONTAL/inclined banded
   drill-core, distinct from the vertical sight-glasses in ChemWorks/BioCore. GOOD.
   BUT it is undercut by anchoring (criterion 8): the whole rack hovers in empty space
   below the building on its own legs, so at thumbnail it still reads as detached gear
   rather than a core sheltered under a lean-to on the shed front.

7. **Attractive?** PARTIAL. The strata cut and the now-banded core are pleasing up close,
   but the silhouette is awkward: a heavy tall block on the left with a long thin tail and
   a separate gadget floating underneath. The dark, cluttered front face and the big `K`
   shadow gap between wall and rack drag the read down at thumbnail size.

8. **Unique built form + opening on LIT-LEFT?** FAIL on form. Per-column profile: the
   left mass (cols 8–32) is ~40–54 px tall, the right shed (cols 34–48) only ~20–26 px.
   So this is a **tall strata block + a low shed tail**, not a single LONG LOW shed. The
   left block is about as wide as it is tall — the squat/tall cube the contract forbids;
   round-0's highest-priority fix ("reproportion to a long low shed, knock 2–3 rows of
   height off") was not applied (the strata block is if anything taller). A box like this
   is an automatic TWEAK. Opening IS on the lit-LEFT face (correct side) — the `o`/`O`
   "CORE LIBRARY" placard reads — but below it the door is a dark `K`/`d`/`D` jumble with
   scattered `c`/`h` specks, not a crisp `K`-framed roll-up panel. Door unresolved.

## Round-0 issue resolution

1. **Squat gateway cube → long low mono-pitch shed:** NOT RESOLVED. Still a tall, roughly
   cubic strata block with a low shed glued to its right; no overall long-low read.
2. **Core had no bands & floated detached:** PARTIALLY RESOLVED. Bands now read (4–6 ruled
   segments in the glass case) — good. But the rack is still detached, free-standing in
   the void below the mass on its own legs, not anchored to the building.
3. **Lit-left door was a dark opening → crisp framed door:** NOT RESOLVED. Placard reads,
   but the door itself is still a dark cluttered opening, not a framed roll-up panel.

## Tweak list (painter-actionable)

1. **Collapse the tall strata block into the shed height (highest priority).** The left
   mass tops out near y≈1 while the shed tops near y≈20 — kill that ~18px height
   difference. Drop the strata block's roofline down so its top sits at roughly the same
   height as the shed's high (back) edge, and stretch the footprint along `wx`. Target a
   single horizontal mass ~much longer than tall (aim left+right combined width ≈ 2.5–3×
   the wall height). Knock ~6–8 rows of wall/strata height off the left block; the strata
   cut should be a low BANK behind/beside the shed, not a tower the shed leans on.

2. **Re-anchor the core rack ONTO the building.** Remove the free-standing legs (rows
   51–54) and the `K` shadow gap (rows 41–42) between wall and rack. Mount the rack on a
   `d` lean-to shelf that visibly attaches to the front-left wall, under a thin `D`/`M`
   canopy on two `d` posts — the core should sit IN the structure's shadow, touching the
   front face, not hovering in open space below the drop-shadow.

3. **Make the main roof a real mono-pitch slope.** The left block's top is a flat lid
   carrying strata + `r`/`o` specks. Either (a) move the strata onto the CUT FACE (the
   vertical back-left bank) and give the shed one continuous tilted roof plane high at
   back-left → low at front-right, with 3–4 straight `d` seam lines running down-slope; or
   (b) if the strata stays as a berm top, clearly tilt the shed roof separately so the
   silhouette reads pitched, not flat-topped.

4. **Promote the LEFT door from "opening" to a crisp framed door.** On the lit-left face,
   frame a wide `K`-outlined roll-up panel: lighten the panel to `M` (it's currently
   reading near `d`/`K`), add 2–3 horizontal `d` roll-segment lines, one `c` glass strip,
   and add a 1px `L` highlight on the lit (left) jamb so the frame pops at thumbnail. Keep
   the `o`/`O` "CORE LIBRARY" placard (it already reads). Remove the scattered diagonal
   `c`/`h` specks that currently clutter the door area.

5. **Let the strata bank visibly meet the shed.** Make the strata cut butt into the shed's
   back-left wall (a `K` retaining lip along the seam) so it reads as "carved into the
   hill," reinforcing the unique form instead of two stacked boxes.

  would read cleaner once the rack is re-anchored.
