3. **Front corner faces viewer?** PASS. As round cylinder drums there is no hard corner; the front-facing curve plus top-down rim ellipses present a correct 3/4 view. Acceptable for the cylinder form.

4. **Sharp / in-palette / clean K outline / glass vs louvers?** PASS. 13 opaque colors sampled, every hex matches the MindFrame set exactly — no anti-alias, no off-palette, no strays. Hard K outline throughout. The mid-drum and lower-right panes read as GLASS: vertical `h`/`O`/`c` sheen highlights inside K-mullion frames with sills, not black-barred panels. The right-face windows now carry a consistent framed cadence (lit pane / K bar / lit pane).

5. **Cohesive palette + motifs?** PASS. Muted indigo/violet walls (`M`/`D`/`d`), magenta accent (`o`/`O`) on tier rims, oculus ring, tie-threads, dial and ground spill; data-screen glow + observation-glass + subtle compute-station motifs all present. Calm-institute premium tone holds.

6. **One-of-one signature (FLAT swarm plane, dial, NOT an orb)?** PASS. It is a flat horizontal parallelogram with a clear `g`/`G` mid-axis — a top-down floor of dots, NOT a sphere/ring/orb, so the collision rule holds and it stays distinct from Volt's orb / BioCore's ring / ChemWorks' molecule. The dot-swarm now resolves into brighter `O`/`h` flocking clusters (a bright clump toward the dial edge, pink clumps along the lower edge) over the dimmer field, with thin `o`/`g` tie-threads. The **optimization dial** is now a genuine instrument: a small K-outlined circular gauge floating off the plane's right edge with a bright `o`/`h` body and a needle, clearly separated from the dot field — exactly the round-0 ask. Legible and one-of-one.

7. **Attractive / reads as PREMIUM?** PASS. The muddy zones are resolved; the piece now reads polished and monumental — clearly the showpiece rather than unresolved. Strong silhouette, controlled glow, refined detailing.

8. **Unique built form + opening on RIGHT + glowing?** PASS. Tiered round ziggurat with open oculus, not the gateway cube — the most dramatic silhouette in the set. Glow is measurably concentrated on the RIGHT (35 glow px right vs 2 left across the base band) — control room + opening correctly on the assigned shaded RIGHT and glowing.
