# Review: Longitudinal Study Wing (mindframe) — round 0

**VERDICT: TWEAK**

The built form, glass ribbon, and palette are strong, and the LEFT half of the rooftop timeline (Y1–Y2 medallions + the climbing magenta line) reads beautifully. But the hero signature breaks down across its RIGHT half: the center Y3 medallion is a dark `K`/`G` blob (reads as a hole, not a portrait disc), the trend-line dissolves into a sparse scatter of pink pixels instead of a continuous climb to a clear Y5 endpoint, the advancing participant token is not distinguishable from the medallions, and the Year 1→5 waypoints don't read as 5 evenly-spaced ordered ticks. Because the timeline IS the one-of-one hero, its legibility must hold end-to-end — hence TWEAK.

1. **Iso 2:1?** PASS. Top edges and the eave-line descend ~1px vertical per 2px horizontal; the long left glazed face and the right shaded face both ride a clean 2:1 grid (e.g. eave rows 18→24, ribbon corners step consistently).

2. **Roof visible 3/4 top-down?** PASS. The `M` roof plane is clearly visible across the spine (rows ~16–26, cols 24–47), with the raised `D` ridge running its length — good top-down read.

3. **Front corner faces viewer?** PASS. The near vertical corner (col ~6, the left wing edge) faces the viewer; lit-LEFT glazed face and shaded-RIGHT depth face split off it correctly.

4. **Sharp / clean / in-palette / clean K outline / no strays — AND glass reads as GLASS?** MOSTLY PASS. Single-pixel `K` outline is continuous; colors are all in the 13-char set; no off-palette/blur. The four left-face bays DO read as glass: violet `g`/`G` cores with `L`/`c`/`W` sheen and `K` frame, not black-barred (see ribbon crop). Minor: the rightmost stretch of the trend-line leaves isolated `o`/`O` singletons floating on the bare `M` roof (rows ~17–25, cols ~30–40) that look like strays rather than a line — clean these up (see tweak 2).

5. **Cohesive MindFrame palette + motifs?** PASS. Muted indigo/violet `M`/`D` walls, magenta `o`/`O` accent on the eave strip + trend-line, observation-glass ribbon, soft-glow readout — all on-brand and clearly a MindFrame sibling.

6. **One-of-one signature present + legible + distinct?** PARTIAL — the reason for TWEAK. Distinct concept (horizontal TIME track w/ portraits + retention curve — not TerraBase's exploded layers, not a bell/pie/fork): YES. Present: YES. Legible end-to-end: NO.
   - **Y1–Y2 medallions** (cols ~10–18, rows ~13–18): read well — `M`/`L` rim, `d` head silhouette, soft `O` glow. 
   - **Y3 medallion** (center roof, cols ~22–27, rows ~20–24): FAIL — a dark `K`/`G`/`d` blob with no bright rim or `O` glow; reads as a roof hole/shadow, not a portrait disc (painter's self-noted weak spot confirmed).
   - **Retention trend-line:** strong climb Y1→Y2 on the left, then DISSOLVES into scattered `o`/`O` pixels across the mid/right roof — never resolves into one continuous climbing line reaching a clear Y5 high point.
   - **Advancing token:** NOT distinguishable — lost in the busy patch around Y3; no readable bright `O`/`h` token + motion trail standing apart from the medallions.
   - **Year 1→5 ticks:** do not read as 5 evenly-spaced, ordered waypoints L→R; the right two waypoints are absent/illegible.
   - **"Retention %" readout** on the end annex: not legible.

7. **Attractive?** PASS overall — pleasing palette, nice glow, good silhouette; the messy right-roof patch is the only thing holding it back aesthetically.

8. **UNIQUE BUILT FORM + opening on assigned LIT-LEFT?** PASS. Genuinely a long low horizontal wing with a taller end-annex cube at far-back-right (records/archive, single small `g` window at cols ~44–46, rows ~36–39) — emphatically NOT the gateway cube and distinct from MindFrame siblings. The hero glazing (4 rhythmic cohort-office bays) is on the LIT-LEFT face as assigned; shaded-right face stays plainer. Correct.

## Numbered tweak list (painter-actionable)

1. **Rebuild the Y3 medallion as a lit portrait disc.** Replace the dark `K`/`G`/`d` blob at center roof (≈cols 22–27, rows 20–24) with the same recipe as Y1/Y2: a `M`/`L` circular rim, a `d` head silhouette inside, and an `O` glow halo so it reads as a retained-cohort portrait — not a hole. Keep it the same diameter as the other medallions for an even row.

2. **Make the retention trend-line one continuous climbing stroke.** From Y1 (left, low) to Y5 (right, high), draw an unbroken `o`-core / `O`-highlight line that visibly slopes UP across all 5 waypoints. Remove the isolated `o`/`O` singletons currently floating on the bare `M` roof (≈cols 30–40, rows 17–25) — consolidate them into the line so it reads as a curve, not confetti. End it on a clear high point above Y5.

3. **Lay out 5 evenly-spaced Y1→Y5 waypoint ticks on the ridge.** Add tiny `o` tally marks / `K` ticks on the `D` ridge beam at 5 even intervals L→R, each sitting under its medallion, so the year-axis ordering is unmistakable. Right now only ~2–3 waypoints read.

4. **Give the advancing token a distinct, brighter read.** Place one bright `O`/`h` token mid-track (e.g. between Y3 and Y4) ON the trend-line, 1–2px larger/brighter than surrounding pixels, with a short faint `o`/`c` motion trail pointing toward Y5. It must stand apart from the medallions — currently it's invisible in the busy patch.

5. **Add 1–2 fading (attrition) medallions toward Y4–Y5.** Per the blueprint, dim the later-year medallions (lower-`M`/`d`, no `O` glow) vs. the glowing retained ones — this both fills the empty right roof and sells the "retention over time" story while keeping the disc shapes legible.

6. **De-clutter the patch where the line meets the roof edge** (≈cols 30–36, rows 22–28). Several `O`/`h`/`c` pixels collide with the roof eave and the end-annex top corner, muddying the read. Thin them so the trend-line, the eave strip, and the annex corner each stay distinct.

7. **(Optional) Surface the "retention %" readout on the end annex.** A tiny `o`/`c` 2–3px glyph on the lit upper face of the far-back annex would pay off the optimization-signal motif; skip if it crowds the silhouette.
