# Review: Deep Earth Observatory (terrabase) — round 2

**VERDICT: SHIP**

Premium showpiece, re-reviewed after the light round-2 polish. Confirmed SHIP with no
regressions — all three polish items landed cleanly and the signature/silhouette are intact.

## Per-criterion

1. **Iso 2:1?** PASS. Base reads as a proper 2:1 isometric diamond; deck/wall edges follow the
   2:1 slope. The tall derrick rises on the vertical axis above the diamond without breaking the
   iso footprint.
2. **Base roof visible?** PASS. The rig-house deck (the equipment base top) is visible 3/4
   top-down — the borehole collar, the o/O hazard-stripe placard band, and the draw-works element
   all sit on the readable deck plane.
3. **Front corner faces viewer?** PASS. The front corner of the base diamond points at the viewer;
   the lit LEFT face and the shaded RIGHT face splay away to either side correctly.
4. **Sharp / clean / in-palette / clean K outline + clean thin lattice / no strays?** PASS.
   Palette audit: 13 real sprite colors, every one mapping exactly to a blueprint char
   (K W L M D d h c g G o O r) — the only two "extra" colors in the render are the
   transparency-checkerboard grey/white, not sprite pixels. Hard K outline throughout. The derrick
   is a genuinely thin, open, see-through lattice — converging K/D legs with d cross-bracing rungs,
   negative space between members, no blobbing and no stray pixels.
5. **Cohesive TerraBase palette + motifs?** PASS. Sand/steel tans (W/L/M/D/d) + mineral-teal sensor
   glow (h/c/g/G) + amber core/hazard (o/O/r). Motifs all honored: literal drill-rig derrick +
   borehole + draw-works, layered rock strata (the planetary cutaway), weatherproof instruments
   (descending probe, surface gauge, work-light), teal sensor readouts, o hazard striping.
6. **One-of-one signature present + legible + distinct?** PASS. The cutaway is the hero and it
   reads: a glowing K-walled shaft drops from the collar through the planet's concentric interior —
   rust/mantle r/o → amber outer-core o/O → near-white W hottest band → c/h teal inner-core glint
   at center (amber→teal gradient, exactly per blueprint). The descending probe on its line + amber
   sensor blip and the rising g/c data-trace with the surface gauge are both present. Distinct from
   #4 Geological Core Lab — this is the whole planet's live interior down a borehole, not a sample
   on a rack.
7. **Attractive / reads as PREMIUM showpiece?** PASS. Tallest, most dramatic, most open silhouette
   of the set; the converging lattice + crown work-light + glowing borehole + planetary cutaway make
   it unmistakably the hero of the catalog.
8. **Unique built form + opening on RIGHT + glowing on shaded side?** PASS. Derrick-over-glowing-shaft
   form, not the gateway cube. Opening is on the assigned RIGHT (shaded) face: a K-framed control
   window glowing with bright o/O interior, teal g/c instrument readouts up top, and h reflections —
   so it reads clearly on the shaded side. The LIT LEFT face is correctly plainer (single c window +
   draw-works), keeping the contrast right.
