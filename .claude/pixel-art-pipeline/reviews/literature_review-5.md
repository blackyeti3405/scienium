# Review — Literature Review (gateway) · round 5

**Verdict: TWEAK**

Reference: `art_ref/mockups/literature_review.png`
Render: `art/preview/literature_review_generic.png`

## Verdict rationale
The render nails the *shell* of the drawing — an open two-story reading
pavilion, open glass canopy on top, orange columns, arched entrance, side
awning — but it drops the one feature the blueprint names as the focal
literature tell: the **open books splayed on the balcony**. Without them the
building reads as a generic iso two-storey box, not a *reading* pavilion. That
alone is an automatic TWEAK. Compounding it, the drawing's purple/green
"book" coloring has been swapped to teal everywhere, so even the shelf band and
upper window no longer read as literature.

## What matches (keep)
- **Silhouette / massing:** two-story open pavilion, upper box set back on a
  wider lower box, front corner to viewer. Reads as the drawing's stack.
- **Open glass canopy (focal #1):** open rectangular frame + purple diamond
  skylight + teal cross-hatched glass on the far slope tilted up to the
  back-right. This is the strongest part of the render — faithful.
- **Orange support columns** between the levels — present and well placed.
- **Arched entrance** at the front-right with orange trim — clean match.
- **Shelf band** on the lower front-left and a **side awning on thin posts**
  projecting right — both present in the right locations.
- **Base gates all pass:** true 2:1 iso, roof visible, front corner forward,
  crisp pixels with a clean K outline (no blur/AA/mush), on the generic
  palette, canopy glass reads as glass.

## Fidelity gaps (fix these)
1. **Open books on the balcony are MISSING (focal literature tell, blueprint
   priority 2).** The drawing has a big open book splayed front-and-center on
   the balcony ledge — butterfly/V silhouette, a center spine, purple/green
   pages. The render has only two small teal squares at that junction. This is
   the redo's whole point and it's absent.
2. **Purple/green literature coloring lost to teal.** The reference uses
   purple/green (D/d + g/G) on the book pages, the upper-right window, and the
   shelf band; the render makes all of those teal. Teal should be reserved for
   the actual glass canopy — the books/window/shelf should carry the
   green+purple tint so they read as literature, not glass.
3. **Upper front-right window reads as a teal crystal/gem, not a tinted
   window.** The drawing shows a rectangular purple/green-tinted window pane.
   The render's irregular teal blob reads as a gemstone.
4. **Upper front-left face has horizontal slats, but the drawing has
   diagonal-hatched blinds/striped awning.** Change the hatch direction to
   diagonal.
5. **Side awning is a solid teal box; the drawing's is a diagonal-hatched
   angled overhang** on thin posts. Give it slatted/striped hatching and an
   angled (sloped) top edge.
6. **Upper box sits a touch too centered.** In the drawing it's offset toward
   the back-left with the balcony + books projecting forward at front-center.
   Minor, but nudging it back-left would improve the read.

## Priority for the redo
Get the **open books** on the balcony first (they are the identity), then swap
the literature accents (books/window/shelf) from teal to purple/green. Items
4–6 are polish once the books read.
