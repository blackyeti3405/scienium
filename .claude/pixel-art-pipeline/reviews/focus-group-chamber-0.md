# Review: Focus Group Chamber (mindframe) — round 0
**VERDICT: TWEAK**

Strong, distinct build: a genuine round open-oculus amphitheater pit with the magenta word-cloud hovering above — unmistakably not the gateway cube and not any MindFrame sibling. It falls short of SHIP on three painter-flagged reads: the **seat ring** is edge-loaded and sparse at the back, the **rising bubbles** read as a particle trail rather than speech-bubbles, and the **curved mirror band** is over-segmented by `K` so it leans toward "barred panel" instead of continuous sheened glass.

## Per-criterion notes

1. **Iso 2:1?** PASS. The cylinder lip ellipse and the entry-stub box follow a clean 2:1 iso. Lip ellipse (rows 27–35) and the front-stub diamond (rows 44–54) are consistent. No skew.

2. **Open top + ring visible (3/4 top-down)?** PARTIAL. The `K`-rimmed oculus is genuinely OPEN and the `d`/`D` sunken bowl reads as a pit (rows 27–36). But the ring of seats is **edge-loaded**: `LML`/`MhM`/`hMM` seat clusters sit on the left/right walls (rows 32–36) while the **back arc of the pit (rows 27–29) is nearly empty** and the **front seats are occluded** by the entry stub — so it reads as "two side benches" more than a full circular ring. Central table glow (`oOOo` row 33, `LoL` row 35) does land.

3. **Front corner/curve faces viewer?** PASS. The drum's front curve faces the viewer and the entry stub sits on the near face (rows 44–54). Good 3/4 read.

4. **Sharp / clean / in-palette / clean K outline / no strays — AND mirror reads as GLASS not black bars?** PARTIAL.
   - Palette: PASS — 13/13 colors, none off-palette, all defined, 64×64 (verified programmatically).
   - Outline: mostly clean single-pixel `K` silhouette; no obvious strays in the body.
   - Mirror band (rows 37–43): reads as glass on the lit-LEFT (`L`/`W`/`c` silvered panes with `O`/`c` sheen) and as darker mirror on shaded-RIGHT (`D`/`d`/`G`). BUT the band is **threaded with too many interior `K` pixels** (e.g. rows 39–42: `KgcccK..KK`, `KLgccKKK...`, `KLLggKKKK...`) which chop the silvered run into segments — drifting toward the "black-barred panel" failure rather than a continuous curved one-way mirror with sweeping sheen.

5. **Cohesive MindFrame palette + motifs?** PASS. Muted indigo/violet drum (`M`/`D`/`d`), magenta accent (`o`/`O` oculus rim + cloud), one-way mirror band, ring seating, soft data glow. On-brand for the calm human-studies institute.

6. **One-of-one signature present + legible + distinct?** PARTIAL.
   - Word-cloud: STRONG. Hovering magenta cluster (rows 8–15) with `W`/`h` word-chips, `G` text, `O`/`o` halo, and two `K`-framed tag labels (rows 8–11) — legible, distinct, clearly "theme tagging." Best part of the piece.
   - Rising bubbles → merge: WEAK. Rows 17–26 are scattered single `c`/`h`/`o`/`O` pixels — they read as a **sparkle/particle trail**, not speech-bubbles (no bubble outline + tail shapes), and there's no clear "one bubble per seat lifting off." The merge-into-cloud narrative is only half-told because the bubbles don't read as bubbles and the seats they should rise from are faint.

7. **Attractive?** PASS. Pleasing silhouette, good glow palette, the floating cloud + sunken pit composition is appealing.

8. **Unique built form + opening reads OPEN with signature in/over the pit?** PASS (form) / PARTIAL (signature-in-pit). The round open-top pit is a genuinely unique built form — NOT a generic box, NOT the gateway cube. Opening is OPEN and the cloud hovers over the pit. Knocked to partial only because the in-pit half of the signature (seats + per-seat bubbles) under-reads.

## Tweak list (numbered, painter-actionable)

1. **Fill the back arc of the seat ring.** Add 3–4 `M`/`L` seat silhouettes across the BACK of the pit (rows 27–29, roughly cols 26–38) so the ring reads all the way around, not just as two side benches. Keep them small and `K`-capped so they read as occupied chairs in shadow.

2. **Make the front seats peek over the stub.** The two front-most chairs are occluded by the entry stub — lift 1–2 `L`/`h` chair-tops 1 row above the stub roof (around rows 43–44, cols 28–36) so the viewer sees the front of the ring, not just the back.

3. **De-bar the mirror band.** In rows 39–42, replace the interior `K` separators inside the silvered run with `g`/`G` (mullions) or `D` (shaded glass) so the curve reads as continuous one-way mirror. Reserve `K` for the band's top/bottom edge only. Add 1–2 diagonal `W`/`h` sheen streaks sweeping across the lit-LEFT panes to sell GLASS.

4. **Turn the trail into actual speech-bubbles.** Replace the scattered single pixels in rows 17–24 with 4–5 small but legible bubbles: a 2×2 `o`/`c` body with a `K` 1-px outline and a tiny `o` tail, each clearly lifting off a seat and angling inward/upward. Fewer, shaped bubbles read far better than many specks.

5. **Anchor each bubble to a seat.** Position the lift-off point of each bubble directly above a ring seat (tie to tweaks 1–2) so the "every participant speaks → themes merge" story is literal, not implied.

6. **(Optional) Brighten the central table.** The `oOOo`/`LoL` table glow (rows 33–35) is faint; one more `O` pixel at the table center would strengthen the "group gathered around a table" focal point under the cloud.
