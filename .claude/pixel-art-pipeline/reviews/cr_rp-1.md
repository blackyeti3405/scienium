# Creative review — Research Plan (gateway) · round 1

**Verdict: TWEAK**

## What works
- **Palette: fully compliant / recolor-safe.** Only the 13 canonical chars (`K W L M D h c g G o O r`; `d` defined-but-unused) appear across all 64 rows, correct 64×64 dims. No off-palette pixels — no auto-TWEAK trigger.
- **Building base is _t5-grade.** Chunky lavender iso roof, crisp K outline, well-shaded teal-glass front windows (h/c highlights), clean drop shadow, side data-cylinders with orange bands. Reads solid and matches the axiom/mindframe hero quality.
- **Strong drafting/engineering identity.** Blueprint side-screens (grid + crosshair + house-plan icons), orange measurement brackets/rulers framing the projection, dotted-orange blueprint rim on the roof edge, projector nozzle + rising beam, W sparkles. The gateway reads unmistakably as a drafting/engineering hall.

## What holds it back (the hero)
1. **The floating wireframe hologram — the intended spectacular hero — reads as the weakest element.** On the grey background the teal edges are single-pixel and broken/dotted (the hexagon's diagonal edges are gapped `c` dots, not continuous lines), so instead of a crisp glowing 3D schematic it reads as a faint asterisk/starburst of scattered noise. Compare mindframe_t5's floating chart: bold, continuous, high-contrast, instantly legible. The star of a CREATIVE _t5 piece cannot be the noisiest, faintest part.
2. **Orphan pixel.** Row 62 has an isolated `K` at col 32, one row *below* the shadow's bottom edge (row 61) — a stray floating dot. Remove.
3. **Beam origin is slightly noisy.** A few orange `O`/`o` pixels sit inside the teal projector burst on the roof (e.g. rows 27, 30), reading as speckle rather than clean glow.

## Bottom line
On-palette and the architecture/base is genuinely _t5-quality, but the wireframe hologram — the whole point of the "glowing hologram hero" brief — is too sparse and broken to read as spectacular. Tighten the hero and clean two stray-pixel blemishes and this ships.
