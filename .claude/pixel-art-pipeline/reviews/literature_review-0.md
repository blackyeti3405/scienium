# Review — Literature Review (generic) · Round 0 · FIDELITY to drawing

**Reference:** `art_ref/mockups/literature_review.png`
**Render:** `art/preview/literature_review_generic.png`

## Verdict: TWEAK

The render passes the *base gates* but is not yet the building in the drawing. It
reads as a generic two-story glass lab, not the open reading pavilion with books
on the balcony. Two of the three named focal features are missing outright.

## Base gates (pass)
- True 2:1 iso, front corner to viewer, top/roof visible. OK.
- Crisp — clean K (#241f38) outline, no blur/AA/mush. OK.
- On the generic palette (lavender body, teal glass, orange accents on grey bg). OK.
- Glass reads as glass (teal windows + cross-hatched canopy). OK.

## Silhouette
Two-story massing is present: a smaller upper volume set back over a wider lower
box. Reasonable. But the upper volume sits too *centered* — in the drawing it is
pushed toward the back-left, leaving the front balcony ledge open for the books.
Right now the front of the balcony is just the flat roof of the lower box.

## Focal features — fidelity check
1. **Open glass canopy (top): PARTIAL / OK.** The teal cross-hatched skylight
   reads as an open glass canopy tilted back. Acceptable — keep it, but ensure the
   frame keeps reading as *see-into-the-room* (open frame), not a filled teal lid.
   Currently borderline.
2. **Open books on the balcony ledge: MISSING (critical).** This is THE literature
   tell and the drawing's focal point — two open-book "butterfly" shapes with
   purple/green pages splayed on the front balcony, dead-center between the two
   stories. The render has nothing there. Automatic TWEAK on its own.
3. **Lower base = shelf band + arched entrance: MISSING.** In the drawing the
   lower level is a *solid base*: a colored horizontal SHELF BAND (green→purple
   stripe) across the front-left face, and a dark ARCHED ENTRANCE with orange
   trim at the front-right. The render instead puts rows of teal "eye" windows on
   both lower faces — this is what makes it read as a *different* building (a glass
   office, not a library base). No arch, no shelf stripe.

## Other named features
- **Orange support columns:** present on the upper level (good) and echoed below. Keep.
- **Upper front-right window (teal tint):** present. OK.
- **Upper front-left diagonal-hatched blinds/awning panels:** MISSING — that face
  is blank lavender with only the orange columns.
- **Side awning to the right:** the render has a flat grey shelf projecting right
  with an orange post, but it reads as a plain slab, not the drawing's
  diagonal-hatched slatted awning on thin posts.

## Net
"A different building than the drawing" on the two decisive counts (no books, no
library base). Base craft is good; the content is wrong. Redo the front balcony
and the lower level and it will land.

## Concrete tweaks (ordered by impact)
1. Add the OPEN BOOKS on the front balcony ledge — two open-book "V"/butterfly
   shapes, purple/green (M/g) pages with a K crease and an orange (o) spine,
   seated on the front-center ledge between the two stories. Non-negotiable focal
   tell.
2. Rebuild the lower level as a SOLID BASE, not a window wall: a horizontal
   colored SHELF BAND (green→purple, g/M) across the front-left face, and a dark
   K ARCHED ENTRANCE with orange (o/r) trim at the front-right face. Drop the rows
   of teal eye-windows.
3. Turn the right-side extension into a diagonal-hatched AWNING on thin orange
   posts (slatted overhang), not a flat grey slab.
4. Add diagonal-hatched blinds/slat panels on the upper front-left face (keep the
   orange columns over them).
5. Nudge the upper canopy volume back toward the back-left so the front balcony
   ledge is clear for the books, and keep the canopy frame reading as open.
