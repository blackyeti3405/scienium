# Review: Geological Core Lab (terrabase) — round 0

**VERDICT: TWEAK**

A clean, in-palette render with an excellent strata hillside, but the built form reads as the forbidden chunky gateway cube (not a long low shed), the hero LEFT door is a dark smudge rather than a crisp door, and the one-of-one inclined banded core fails to read — no stratigraphy bands are legible and the whole signature floats detached on the drop-shadow.

## Per-criterion

1. **Iso 2:1?** PASS. Diamond top and 2:1 edge slopes are correct; angles are consistent.
2. **Roof visible 3/4 top-down?** PARTIAL. A top face is clearly visible, BUT it reads as a flat near-square cube lid with corrugation specks, not the contracted shallow **mono-pitch shed roof**. No high-back/low-front tilt is legible.
3. **Front corner faces viewer?** PASS. The near vertical corner edge points at the viewer; left and right faces both visible.
4. **Sharp/clean/in-palette/clean K outline?** PASS. Verified at source res (64×64): exactly 13 in-palette colors, **zero off-palette / zero anti-alias** sprite pixels, hard `K` silhouette. Technically clean.
5. **Cohesive TerraBase palette + motifs (esp. strata hillside)?** PARTIAL→GOOD on strata, weak elsewhere. The hillside cut (back-left, rows ~16–28) is the best element: legible diagonal bands of `r` rust / `D`/`d` brown / `M` ochre / `L` pale — genuine stratigraphy. But the rest of the building is an undifferentiated `M`/`D` brown mass; the warm `o`/`O` amber accent and mineral-teal glow barely register away from the broken signature.
6. **One-of-one signature present + legible + distinct?** FAIL. The inclined glass-cased banded drill-core does NOT read. The core element (front-center, rows ~46–52) is a thin teal/tan streak sitting on the drop-shadow BELOW the mass — it looks like detached clutter, not a core on a rack under a lean-to. There are NO visible rock-strata bands inside the core (the whole point), and the per-layer scan + mineral label can't be parsed: I can find one `g`/`c` teal scanner blob and one pale `W` label card with a `K` tick + `o` cap, but nothing reads as "scanner lighting band N of a layered core."
7. **Attractive?** PARTIAL. Solid silhouette and the strata cut are pleasing, but the dark front (door + core) is muddy and the floating signature drags the read down at thumbnail size.
8. **Unique built form + opening on LIT-LEFT?** FAIL on form. This reads as the **squat gateway cube**, not a LONG LOW shed — the mass is roughly as tall/deep as it is wide and the roof is flat, exactly the silhouette the contract forbids. Per the rules a generic box is an automatic TWEAK. Opening IS on the lit-left face (correct side), but it's a dark packed "opening," not a crisp hero door (painter's self-noted weak spot confirmed).

## Tweak list (painter-actionable)

1. **Reproportion to a LONG LOW shed (highest priority).** The mass is currently ~cubic. Stretch the footprint along `wx` and cut the height: aim for roughly `Box(wx≈13, wy≈6, h≈7)` so the building is clearly much longer/wider than it is tall. The current `h` is too large — it's what makes it read as a gateway cube. Knock 2–3 rows of wall height off both visible faces (`M` field, `d` base course) and add them to length if needed.

2. **Make the roof a real mono-pitch slope, not a flat lid.** Tilt the top slab so the high edge is back-left and the low edge front-right; render the slope in `L` (lit, upper) grading to `M` (lower), with `d` corrugation seam lines running DOWN-slope (front-right direction). Right now the top is a flat `M`/`L` plane with random `r`/`o` specks reading as gravel — replace those specks with 3–4 straight `d` seam lines to sell corrugation + pitch.

3. **Cut the inclined core's bands so stratigraphy actually reads.** This is the one-of-one and it currently fails. Lay the glass core as a clear horizontal/inclined bar (8–10 px long) on a `D`/`M` two-rail rack under a front lean-to. Inside the `c`/`h` glass case, paint 5–6 distinct 1–2px-wide vertical band segments in order `r` → `D` → `d` → `M` → `L` → `o` so the layered rock is visible end-to-end. Do not let the core blur into one teal smear.

4. **Re-anchor the signature ONTO the building, not the shadow.** The core/scanner/label currently float on the `K` drop-shadow below the front wall, reading as detached debris. Raise the whole rack so it sits on a `d` lean-to shelf attached to the front-left face (under a thin `D`/`M` canopy on two `d` posts), clearly part of the structure.

5. **Make the sliding scanner + per-layer label legible.** Wrap a 2px `g`/`G` mineral-teal collar around ONE band of the core and cast a single bright `c`/`h` light-bar onto that band (make that one band glow brightest `O`/`h`). Above it, a small `W`/`L` label card (3–4px) with a `K` text tick and one `O` dot. Keep the `c` slide-trail to ONE faint segment so it doesn't read as random sparkle.

6. **Promote the hero LEFT door from "opening" to a crisp door.** On the lit-left face, frame a wide `K`-outlined roll-up core-bay door: `M`/`D` panel with 2–3 horizontal `d` roll-segment lines, a `c` glass window strip, and the `o` "CORE LIBRARY" placard kept as a clean 2×3 `o`/`O` block. The area is currently too dark/packed — lighten the door panel to `M` (it's reading near-`d`/`K`) and add a 1px `L` highlight on the lit (left) jamb so the frame pops at thumbnail.

7. **Keep the shaded RIGHT face plainer and let the strata cut meet it.** The right face is fine as a long blank `D` wall; add just one small `d` vent and ensure the strata berm visibly butts into the shed's back so it reads as "carved into the hill," reinforcing the unique form.
