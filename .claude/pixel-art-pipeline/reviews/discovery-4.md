# Review — Discovery (gateway) · round 4

**Reference:** `art_ref/mockups/discovery.png`
**Render:** `art/preview/discovery_generic.png`
**Verdict: TWEAK**

## What matches (keep)
- **Silhouette / massing:** single cubic hub, front corner to viewer, roof visible, ~2:1 iso. Reads as the same basic box as the drawing.
- **Satellite DISH (hero):** unmistakable — large tilted lilac parabolic disc top-left with a straight feed-arm line across it and small tufts/drips at its base. This is the strongest, most faithful element.
- **Cylinder DRUM:** an orange-ringed canister is present at the top-right. Recognizable.
- **Solar/sensor PANEL:** a blue/green diagonal-stripe glazing patch is present on the roof.
- **Orange trim:** roof-edge and base trim bands are present and on-palette.
- On the generic lilac/orange/teal palette; windows read as glass; K outline is mostly clean.

## Why it's still a TWEAK (reads too clean / drifts from the drawing)
The drawing's whole character is a **busy, greebled observatory**; the render's front-left face is nearly bare, so it reads as a *different, cleaner* building than the drawing. The two named front-face features are missing, and a couple of elements are placed/sized wrong.

1. **Ladder/pipe rig is MISSING.** The drawing has a prominent vertical zig-zag framework climbing the far-left front corner — one of the most eye-catching greebles. The render has only a few stray tufts on the left edge; no ladder tower. This is the biggest fidelity gap.
2. **Framed entry/porch is MISSING.** The drawing has a small doorway with a frame in the front-centre of the lit face. The render's lower-front wall is a blank lilac panel — no entry.
3. **No diagonal conduit lines.** The drawing runs red/orange conduit diagonally across the front face; the render only has flat horizontal orange bands. Contributes to the too-clean read.
4. **Solar panel is undersized and drifts inward.** In the drawing it's a distinct tilted rectangle at the right-back of the roof; in the render it's a small mushy stripe crammed near the roof centre between dish and drum.
5. **Drum floats + stray specks.** The drum sits above the roofline like a nub rather than clearly jutting off the right face, and there are detached O/o pixels to its right that break the clean outline.
6. **Right-face window tint.** Drawing shows a purple/green square window low on the right face; render's is teal (minor).

## Concrete fixes (move it toward the drawing)
- Add a **ladder/pipe rig** up the far-left front corner: a 2–3px-wide vertical K zig-zag framework, top of face to base. Single biggest win for the "busy" read.
- Add a **framed entry/porch** in the front-centre of the lit face: a small K-framed doorway at the base band.
- Add 1–2 **diagonal red/orange conduit lines** (use `r`/`o`) across the front-left face instead of only horizontal bands.
- **Enlarge and reposition the solar panel** to a clear tilted rectangle at the right-back of the roof (blue/green diagonal-stripe glazing), separated from the dish and drum.
- Reseat the **drum** so it clearly juts off the upper-right roof edge over the right face (horizontal canister with 2 orange rings), and delete the stray floating specks to its right so the K outline is clean.
- Re-tint the low **right-face window** toward purple/green per the drawing (minor).

Prioritize 1–4 (the greeble + panel) — those are what currently make it read as a different, cleaner building than the drawing.
