# Review: Sampling Station (terrabase) — round 0
**VERDICT: TWEAK**

Judged: `art/preview/buildings/terrabase_sampling-station.png` (640×640 upscale of 64×64 source) against `art/pixel-art-pipeline/blueprints/sampling-station.md`.

## Per-criterion notes

1. **True isometric (2:1)?** Yes. Tarp slab and pad diamond follow clean 2:1 iso edges; no broken-angle stairs. Pass.
2. **3/4 top-down framing, roof/floor visible?** Yes. The full tarp top plane is visible as the dominant top surface, and the packed-earth pad/courtyard is visible below it. Good top-down read. Pass.
3. **Front CORNER faces viewer?** Yes. The near corner of the pad points at the viewer with the two front faces splaying left/right; the front-corner tarp sag is present. Pass.
4. **Sharp — clean K outline, no blur/off-palette?** Mostly pass. Single-pixel `K` outline is crisp, no anti-alias/mush, colors look on-palette (ochre `L/M/D/d`, teal `h/c/g/G`, amber `o/O`, `r` rust). No stray pixels spotted. Minor: the teal ring's lower arc has a couple of nearly-isolated `g` pixels that read slightly noisy, but it's within tolerance.
5. **Cohesive with TerraBase palette + motifs?** Yes. Tarp roof (literal), survey-stripe `o`/`K` ticks ringing the pad, mineral-teal scanner glow, weathered `D`/`d` timber, gold pennant — all honored. Pass.
6. **One-of-one signature present + legible + distinct?** Partial. The rotating sample inside a mineral-teal scanner ring IS present and distinct from siblings (clearly a floating clod in a teal ring, not a petri carousel or jar wall). BUT the **fanned ID-tag arc is under-delivered** — instead of 2–3 discrete holographic label cards arcing out to the right with `K` text ticks + `o` coord dots, there's a single flat white `W` bar to the right of the ring. The "fanning out an arc of ID tags as it identifies the sample" half of the hero does not read. This is the main blocker.
7. **Attractive?** Yes, broadly — strong tarp silhouette, nice teal focal glow against warm ochre, readable pennant. The dark lower mass is a touch heavy/muddy, which dulls it slightly.
8. **Unique built form + opening on assigned side (OPEN)?** Pass with a caveat. It is NOT the gateway cube — the tarp-on-poles awning over an open yard is its own form. The OPEN read works: transparency gaps show between the corner poles on left and right, and the signature equipment sits out in the open court (not behind a glazed face). Caveat: the **lower body is boxy/dark enough that the silhouette flirts with "enclosed plinth"** — the open corners are carried mostly by the upper half. Acceptable, but tightening the lower mass would strengthen it.

## Tweak list (numbered, painter-actionable)

1. **Fan out real ID tags (highest priority — signature fix).** Right of the scanner ring (roughly cols 38–48, rows 26–32 in 64-grid terms, i.e. the upper-right of the ring crop), replace the single flat `W` bar with **2–3 distinct holographic label cards arcing up-and-out**: each a small `W`/`L` rectangle (≈3–4px wide, 2px tall), stepped diagonally so they fan, each separated by 1px of `K` (or empty) so they read as separate cards. Put a 1px `K` "text" tick line inside each card and a single `o` coordinate dot at one corner. Light the top/confirmed card brighter with `O`/`h`. This is what makes it one-of-one — right now it reads as a blank panel.
2. **Rescue the back-left sample crate (painter's noted weak spot — confirmed).** The crate currently dissolves into the dark left wing (cols ~18–26, rows ~26–34). Lighten its visible faces one step (use `M`/`D` boards instead of the `d`/`K` it's sharing with the wing), add **`o` corner brackets** on its top-front edges and a **1px `K` rope-handle loop** on the front face to silhouette it off the wing, and tuck a 1px `K` shadow gap between the crate stack and the wing behind it so the two masses separate. Keep the one open crate showing `r`/`M` rock chunks — that detail is good, just needs the contrast lift to be seen.
3. **Lift the boxy lower mass toward an open depot read.** The lower body (rows ~36–48) is a near-solid dark plinth that pushes the silhouette toward "enclosed cube." Carve 1–2px of negative space (transparent/background) into the front-left and front-right base corners under the open pole bays so daylight reads through beneath the tarp, and re-light the pad floor diamond a step warmer (`L` over the current `M`/`D`) so the courtyard floor reads as an open yard surface rather than a dark box top. This keeps it clearly distinct from the gateway cube.
4. **(Minor) Strengthen the left corner pole.** The right poles read as posts cleanly; the left pole is thin and low-contrast where it meets the wing. Give it one consistent `D` vertical run with a `d` shaded side and a `K` lashing-ring pixel at the roof anchor so all four corner posts read with equal weight — reinforcing the "open on poles, not walled" silhouette.
5. **(Optional polish) Calm the ring's lower arc.** A couple of near-isolated `g` pixels on the lower scanner ring read slightly speckled; connect them into a continuous 1px `g`/`G` arc and keep the rotating-scan `c` ticks to the inner rim so the ring stays a clean torus.
