In the render the cup reads as a **hollow orange oval RING** (lavender shows straight through the middle, so it looks like a badge / mirror / necklace, not a vessel), and the upward fan is only ~2–3 short orange strokes — weak and muddied by an adjacent teal glass patch. The orange chevron is present but sits *below* the ring rather than *on* the cup body. The hero currently does not clearly say "pen cup."
2. **Right unit's top screen reads as a stain, not a screen.** It is a solid dark-teal (`G`) blob filling half the top face, with no frame and no scribble. The drawing's right top is a distinct **framed slanted panel with a colourful scribble** — same species as the left screen. As drawn now it looks like spilled ink, not a drafting screen.
3. **Left shelf band is a thin single-tone teal line.** The drawing's mid band is a fatter, two-tone **green+purple** supply-shelf strip. (No green in the 13-char palette, so approximate green with teal `g`/`G`.) Currently too thin and one-note.
4. **Missing the vertical orange trim post at the back corner** where the two units meet — clearly present in the drawing, absent in the render.

## Tweaks (concrete, all move it toward the drawing)
1. **Rebuild the pen cup as a solid vessel.** Fill the interior — remove the see-through lavender center so it stops reading as an oval ring. Give it a small tapering container body, and move the **orange V-chevron onto the cup's front face** (as in the drawing) rather than floating below it.
2. **Make the pen fan the hero.** Replace the 2–3 short strokes with a prominent spray of **5–6 thin vertical pens/rulers fanning UP and OUTWARD** from the cup mouth: varied heights, a mix of light (`W`/`L`), orange (`o`/`O`), and one teal ruler (`g`), tall enough to rise clearly into the notch. Keep the neighbouring teal glass out of the fan so it stays legible.
3. **Turn the right top into a real slanted screen.** Replace the solid `G` blob with a **K-framed panel + light scribble** (`h`/`c`/`g` strokes on lavender), matching the left screen and the drawing, so it reads as a sketch screen not a spill.
4. **Fatten the left shelf band into a two-tone strip** — a recessed band mixing teal (`g`) with purple (`M`/`D`) to echo the drawing's green+purple supply shelf.
5. **Add a vertical orange (`o`) trim post at the back corner** where the two units meet.
