## Does it hit the _t5 north star? Yes
- **Glowing hologram hero:** a giant floating **open book of teal `h/c` light**
  above the roof — two pages fanning from a lighter central spine, horizontal
  page/text striping that reads as lines on a page. Immediately legible as
  "library / literature" at a glance. This is the catalog's original dream for
  Lit Review and it lands.
- **Beams / streams:** knowledge particles (`W`/`c`/`o` dots) rise from the roof
  toward the book — the axiom-theater beam idea, executed.
- **Sparkles:** teal diamond sparkles scattered around the book, matching the
  `_t5` twinkle.
- **Warm glow:** a warm orange `o/O` glow diamond sits in the center of the roof
  beneath the book — the soft under-glow the blueprint called for.

## Reads as its gateway identity? Yes
Open book + arched reading windows = unmistakably a literature/library hall.
Strongest gateway-identity read of the creative set.

## Iso + outline — clean
- Base is a crisp iso cube: corner-forward, two front faces + top diamond,
  correct 2:1 geometry.
- **Tall arched glass reading windows** (4, glowing teal `h/c/g`) on the front
  faces — genuinely handsome, the highlight of the piece.
- Orange `o/O/r` trim runs the edges; a strong continuous dark-navy `K` outline
  wraps the base cleanly with no jaggies or gaps.
- Row-density scan confirms a well-formed silhouette, smooth taper, no stray
  floating pixels breaking the read.

## Optional polish (non-blocking — all SHIP-worthy as-is)
1. Beam connection between roof and book is a touch thin (rows ~22–24, ~7–10px
   wide). One or two more `c`/`W` particles in that gap would make the book feel
   more clearly "fed" by the rising knowledge stream, like the theater's dense
   beams.
2. The book itself has no dark edge — fine and intentional for a hologram, but a
   faint `g`/`G` darker-teal rim on the outer page edges would give it a hair
   more form against light lab backgrounds.
3. Roof is slightly busy where orange trim, orange particles and teal particles
   overlap; thinning one orange particle would calm it. Minor.

None of these block ship — the sprite is spectacular, cohesive, and on-palette.
