## Why it's not a SHIP yet
Measured against the north-star refs (`axiom_cosmology-visualization-theater_t5`, `terrabase_deep-earth-observatory_t5`), it falls short on the two things the blueprint made the hero ask:

1. **The hologram doesn't read as a swirling GALAXY.** The blueprint specifies "a spiral of h/c stars … small orbiting planet dots." What renders is a static, horizontally-elongated **faceted diamond/lens** — no spiral arms, no bright galactic core, no orbit. It reads as a cut gem, not a galaxy.
2. **The particle field is sparse.** The `_t5` refs fill a large swath of sky with dozens of swirling `c/h` + `o` sparkles that sell the sense of scale and drama. Here there are only ~6–8 stray pixels around a compact shape, so it feels contained rather than spectacular.
3. **Identity is ambiguous — reads as a potion vat, not an observatory.** The connector between building and hologram is a dark teal cylinder with an orange band that looks like a **chimney/spout emitting steam**, not a telescope firing a light-beam. There's no legible telescope and no bright beam column, so the "observatory" read is weak and risks looking like a chemistry building.

Secondary: the domed base is comparatively plain — large flat `M` faces with a single diagonal highlight, versus the banded/riveted/windowed surfaces in the `_t5` refs. The satellite dish also floats with a visible gap from the roof.

## Tweaks (prioritized)
1. **Rework the hero into an actual spiral galaxy:** a bright `W/h` core, 2 curved `c→g` arms sweeping outward, a few `o/O` warm nebula knots, and 3–4 detached `c` "planet" dots on orbit rings. Make it clearly rotate, not a symmetric gem.
2. **Densify + expand the sparkle field** to roughly double the current count and spread it wider/higher so the sky reads busy and dramatic like the `_t5` refs.
3. **Turn the connector into a real light-beam + telescope:** a short angled `L/D` telescope barrel on the roof, firing a bright vertical column with an `h/W` core (not a solid dark cylinder) up into the galaxy. Drop the steam-spout look.
4. **Add surface interest to the dome/base:** an `o` banding line or 1–2 teal-glow windows and a few rivet/`D` shading breaks so the body matches `_t5` density.
5. **Anchor the satellite dish** to the roofline (short strut) so it doesn't float, and let its `c` signal arc echo the galaxy's teal.
