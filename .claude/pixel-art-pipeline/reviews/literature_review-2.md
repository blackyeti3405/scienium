# Review — Literature Review (gateway), round 2

**Reference:** `art_ref/mockups/literature_review.png`
**Render:** `art/preview/literature_review_generic.png`
**Verdict: TWEAK**

## What matches (keep it)
- **Silhouette / stagger:** Reads as a two-story pavilion — a smaller upper box set back over a
  larger forward lower box, front corner pointing at the viewer. Broad massing matches the drawing.
- **Open glass canopy (co-focal):** The top is genuinely OPEN — you see into the lavender interior,
  and the back-right slope carries a cyan cross-hatched glass canopy with grid lines. Faithful and
  recognizable; the strongest part of the render.
- **Orange support columns:** Present on both upper faces and between levels.
- **Upper front-right window:** Cyan diamond glass panel, reads clearly as glass.
- **Side awning:** Dark diagonal overhang projecting right on two hanging orange posts — matches the
  reference side extension.
- **Lower shelf band + arched entrance:** Diagonal shelf stripes on the lower-left face; a dark arch
  with orange trim posts at the lower-right. Both present.
- **Base gates pass:** ~2:1 iso, open roof/top visible, crisp K (#241f38) outline, no blur/AA, on
  the generic mauve+teal+orange palette, glass reads as glass.

## Why it's still a TWEAK (fidelity gaps)
1. **The open books — the literature tell and named co-focal — are NOT recognizable.** At the
   balcony center the render is flat lavender panels split by dark vertical bars with a few stray
   teal dashes (verified at 5x crop). There is no open-book "butterfly": no central spine with two
   splayed pages tilting outward. In the drawing this is the single biggest, most obvious feature —
   two large open books with striped pages sitting proud on the ledge. A viewer would not read
   "books," which for a *Literature Review* gateway is the identity of the building.
2. **Upper front-LEFT face is plain.** The drawing puts diagonal-hatched slatted blinds / a striped
   awning there (mirroring the window on the right). The render leaves that face blank lavender, so
   the upper level looks bare on the left.
3. **Muddy hangings below the balcony.** Light shapes with teal edges drip below the ledge and read
   as icicles/curtains, competing with and burying the books.
4. **Accent color is teal-only.** The reference pages and shelf band are two-tone green+purple; the
   render uses teal streaks throughout, so books, shelves, and glass all share one accent and don't
   separate.

## Concrete, painter-actionable tweaks (move it toward the drawing)
1. **Rebuild the open books as the clear central focal.** Two book "tents" sitting UP on the balcony
   ledge, each a shallow V with a dark **K** center spine and two splayed pages angling outward; make
   each ~10–14px wide so it reads at a glance. Give the pages a **W** top highlight and a **K**
   baseline shadow so they sit proud of the ledge (objects on a shelf), not flush like windows.
2. **Two-tone the pages and the lower shelf band** — alternate **M** (purple) and **g** (teal)
   stripes instead of teal-only, so books/shelves separate from the glass and echo the reference's
   green+purple pages.
3. **Add the diagonal-hatched blinds panel** to the upper front-LEFT face: K/teal diagonal hatch
   lines like the slatted blinds in the drawing, to balance the right-side window.
4. **Clean up the drips** below the balcony ledge — remove the hanging teal-edged blobs so the books
   are the unambiguous read along the front.
5. Keep the canopy, columns, side awning, arch, and overall massing as-is.
