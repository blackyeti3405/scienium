# Review: Seismograph Array (terrabase) — round 0

**VERDICT: TWEAK**

A solid, grounded bunker mass with a genuinely strong shock-ripple — but the hero ink-drum reads as a flat placard/sign (the painter's self-noted weak spot is real), the trace inside it drifts toward the forbidden CRT-oscilloscope read, and the LEFT opening has no legible armored door + ribbon window. Two-to-three targeted fixes and this ships.

## Per-criterion notes

1. **Iso 2:1?** PASS. Clean 2:1 isometric projection; edges follow the correct slopes, no skew.

2. **Roof visible 3/4 top-down?** PASS. The flat parapet deck is fully visible from a 3/4 top-down angle and carries both signature props — good top-plane real estate.

3. **Front corner faces viewer?** PASS. The near vertical corner (~col 30 in 64-space, the bright LIT-LEFT / SHADED-RIGHT split) points at the viewer correctly.

4. **Sharp/clean/in-palette/clean K outline?** MOSTLY PASS. Single-pixel hard `K` outline, no blur/anti-alias, palette looks in-range. Minor: the bottom-front silhouette is a touch noisy/ragged where the strata bands and the cast shadow meet (lower-center), and a couple of stray `K` pixels float on the front-left ground apron near the foot.

5. **Cohesive TerraBase palette + motifs (esp. strata base)?** PASS. Earthy ochre/slate `M`/`D` concrete walls, warm `r`/amber strata, mineral-teal glow — squarely TerraBase. The horizontal rust/`r` + `D`/`d` strata banding stepping the base IS present and is the best strata read of the set so far. Weak spot: it banded the standard box footprint rather than building the half-sunk **berm** — the ground does not rise against the back-left base, so it reads "striped box" more than "half-sunk into bedrock."

6. **One-of-one signature present + legible + distinct?** PARTIAL — the main reason for TWEAK.
   - **Shock-ripple: PASS and excellent.** Concentric `c`/`g` teal rings around an `O`/`r` epicenter marker, with the outer ring lit brighter `O`. Reads clearly as a radiating ground ripple with a predicted next jolt — fully distinct from anything in the catalog. Keep as-is.
   - **Ink drum: FAIL the intent.** The drum is a flat, square-cornered, flat-topped panel standing upright on the roof — it reads as a placard/sign/poster, NOT a rounded rotating paper cylinder. There is no curved top edge, no side-wrap shading, no rotation seam. The painter's self-noted concern is confirmed.
   - **Trace: drifts toward the forbidden read.** The teal zig-zag floating inside the flat panel currently reads like the Electronics-Bench CRT oscilloscope waveform-on-a-screen — exactly the thing the brief says to avoid. Because the drum isn't a drum, the trace has nothing to "wrap," so it looks like a screen graphic.

7. **Attractive?** PASS. Good weight, pleasing palette, the ripple is genuinely eye-catching. Held back only by the placard-drum and the muddy front-foot silhouette.

8. **Unique built form + opening on LIT-LEFT face?** BUILT FORM PASS / OPENING FAIL.
   - Built form: this is convincingly a low, heavy, ground-hugging bunker with strata banding — NOT the gateway cube, and lower/heavier than its siblings. Good.
   - Opening: the assigned LEFT (lit) face shows scattered teal sensor glow dots and one amber placard, but there is **no legible armored door + ribbon window** — no `K`-framed entrance reads. The hero entrance requirement on the lit-left face is unmet. Parapet lip and the 2–3 `K`-rimmed sensor wells in the roof deck are also not clearly present.

## Tweak list (painter-actionable)

1. **Rebuild the ink drum as a real rounded rotating cylinder** (roof, drum prop currently at ~rows 8–22 / cols 22–34 in 64-space, upper-left of roof deck). Replace the flat-topped square panel: draw an upright `cylinder` — give it a **curved/elliptical top cap** (2px `K` rim arcing across the top, `L` highlight on the upper-left of the curve) and **vertical side-wrap shading** (`W` paper highlight on the lit-left third → `L` mid → `M`/`D` on the shaded-right third) so it reads as a round paper drum. Add a faint vertical `d` **rotation seam** down the wrap. Keep the `K` mounting frame at its base.

2. **Make the trace wrap the drum, not float like a screen.** Redraw the `g`/`G` zig-zag seismic trace as a thin jagged line that **follows the curved paper surface** (one continuous horizontal band hugging the cylinder's mid-wrap, bending slightly with the curve), with the live tip a single brighter `c`/`h` pixel. Add the thin `D`/`K` **stylus arm** hanging from above onto the drum (a 1px `D` arm + `K` pivot) so the read is "mechanical pen drawing on rotating paper," not "waveform on a CRT."

3. **Add the LEFT hero entrance** (LIT-LEFT face, ~rows 24–40 / cols 8–28 in 64-space). Cut a clear `K`-framed **armored door** (tall `M`/`D` panel, 2px `K` frame, an `o` handle/bolt detail) plus a narrow horizontal **ribbon window** of `c` glass above or beside it, and the `o` "EPICENTER" placard plate. Pull the loose teal sensor dots back so the door is the dominant element on that face.

4. **(Optional but recommended) Build the half-sunk berm.** Raise 2–3 ochre/rust strata layers (`r` → `D` → `d`, lightest on top) against the **back-left base** so the bunker looks dug into rising ground, not a striped freestanding box. This is the set's strongest-strata building — lean into it.

5. **(Polish) Add the parapet lip + sensor wells.** Give the roof deck a 2px raised `K`-edged `L`/`M` **parapet lip** around its perimeter so the flat roof reads as a walled deck. Drop 2–3 small `K`-rimmed circular **sensor caps** flush into the front roof deck, each with a faint `c` glow dot (the buried-geophone array).

6. **(Polish) Clean the front-foot silhouette.** Remove the stray `K` pixels on the front-left ground apron and tidy the ragged bottom edge where the strata bands meet the cast shadow (lower-center), so the bunker base reads as one crisp grounded silhouette.
