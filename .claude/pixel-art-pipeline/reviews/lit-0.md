# Literature Review — Image-Match Review (round 0)

**Reference:** `art_ref/mockups/literature_review.png`
**Render:** `art/preview/literature_review_generic.png`
**Verdict:** TWEAK

## Palette
PASS — grid uses only canonical chars (D G K L M O W c d g h o r, `.`=transparent). No off-palette chars.

## Silhouette / massing (graded first)
Two-tier stepped massing is roughly right: lower base block front-left + upper block set back-right. Iso is crisp and clean. BUT the single most dominant feature of the reference — the large **open rectangular roof frame** (an open, cross-braced tray/pergola tilted up and back over the top third of the sketch) — is not reproduced. The render's top is a mostly-solid roof slab with a small recessed grey panel plus a little teal angled window. The open, airy frame that defines the reference's silhouette is missing, so the top third reads wrong. This alone is a TWEAK per the shape-first rule.

## Focal features + accents
- **Open book (front deck):** Reference has an unmistakable open book — two splayed pages in a shallow V, front-and-center on the mid deck. The render puts a solid teal/white blocky cube there that reads as a monitor/screen, not a book. Weak — needs to read as pages.
- **Side awnings + dangling legs:** GOOD — striped louvered awnings on left and right with thin dangling support legs are present and match well.
- **Orange support posts:** GOOD — two orange posts on the upper front are present. In the reference they clearly flank a central window; render's central element is teal glass, so the "two orange posts around a window" grouping is only half-legible.
- **Base archway:** GOOD — dark archway with orange trim at the bottom-right base matches.
- **Color:** Render leans heavily on bright teal/cyan. Reference is muted grey with rust/orange as the main accent and only faint purple/green pencil shading. The teal over-reads and steals emphasis from the orange columns.

## Concrete tweaks
1. Rebuild the rooftop as an OPEN rectangular frame/tray: an empty box rim tilted back with a visible inner floor and one or two thin cross-braces — not a solid slab with an inset panel. This is the dominant silhouette element and must be restored.
2. Make the front-deck object read as an OPEN BOOK: two light (W/L) splayed pages forming a shallow tent with a darker spine down the middle and a few short page-line ticks. Replace the teal monolithic cube.
3. Dial back the bright teal (c/g): keep it only as small window-glass accents so the orange (o/O) support posts stay the primary accent, matching the reference's rust columns.
4. Make the upper-front window read as a single glass panel flanked by the two orange posts (post–window–post), as in the reference, instead of a teal block.
