## Tweak list (painter-actionable)

1. **Concentric interior layers — make them DISTINCT glowing bands.** In the cutaway shaft below the borehole collar (roughly the bottom 14–16 rows, centered on the shaft column), replace the current stacked amber deck rows with clearly nested concentric arcs, top→bottom: crust `D`/`r` → mantle `r`/`o` → outer-core `O`/`o` (hottest, brightest, widest) → a small inner-core `c`/`g` glint at the very tip. Each band should be at least 2px tall and curve (concentric), not run as flat horizontal floor lines, so they read as planet layers and not building floors. This is the hero — give it the most saturation contrast in the sprite.

2. **Descending probe — make one legible capsule.** Place a single distinct `W`/`c` probe capsule (2×3px) partway down the shaft, ON the center `D` draw-line, with one blinking `O` sensor pixel. Surround it with `K` so it pops off the glow. Right now it cannot be picked out from the checker noise.

3. **Rising prediction — separate it from the probe line.** Run a thin `g`/`c` data-trace UP one side of the shaft (not overlapping the probe's `D` line) with 2–3 small rising `O` reading-blips, terminating at a `K`-framed surface gauge on the deck near the collar whose needle climbs to a bright `O` predicted mark. Currently the rise reads as the same dashed column as the descent — give them opposite sides/colors (descent = `D` line + `W`/`c` probe; rise = `g`/`c` trace + `O` blips).

4. **Clean the shaft noise.** Remove the scattered loose `c`/`g`/`K` checker pixels in the upper-center shaft (visible in the right-face crop) that currently read as random static; keep only the deliberate probe, line, and trace from tweaks 2–3.

5. **Glow halo (premium polish).** Add a subtle `O`/`c` glow halo ring around the borehole collar on the deck so the "live borehole" reads, and so the eye is drawn down the shaft rather than to the amber deck rows.

6. **Optional — `o` DEEP EARTH placard.** Add the small `o` "DEEP EARTH" placard / hazard plate on the rig-house right face called for in the blueprint (a 1–2px `o` stripe with `K` frame) to reinforce the premium signage. Optional, not blocking.
