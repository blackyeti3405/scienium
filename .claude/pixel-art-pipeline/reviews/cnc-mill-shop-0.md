# Review — CNC Mill Shop (round 0)

**VERDICT: TWEAK**

## Per-criterion
1. **ISOMETRIC 2:1 — PASS.** Box walls follow a clean 2:1 isometric projection; left and right faces recede correctly.
2. **3/4 top-down, roof visible — PASS.** The roof diamond is fully visible and well-proportioned above the two wall faces.
3. **Front corner faces viewer — PASS.** The vertical front corner edge sits center-bottom; left and right walls splay away from it.
4. **SHARP / clean K outline — FAIL.** The silhouette outline is clean (single-pixel `K`), and the sprite is palette-clean (13 chars, no stray/off-palette pixels — the only non-palette values are the transparency checkerboard). BUT the rooftop border between the glass box and the eaves (JSON rows ~20–28) is noisy: scattered `L`/`D`/`o`/`d` single pixels read as speckle/mush instead of clean corrugated ribs. The corrugation is not legible as parallel ribbed lines.
5. **Cohesive with Volt Lab palette/motifs — PASS (mostly).** Steel-grey body, cyan glass, electric-yellow accents all on-palette. Yellow eave chevrons and the diagonal yellow power-line stripes on the walls land the motif. Conduit/cable drape and rivet dots are not clearly readable but the overall family fit is good.
6. **One-of-one signature legible (SUBTRACTIVE milling) — FAIL (the headline problem).** The rooftop reads as a generic HVAC/rooftop unit with two cyan skylights, not a CNC mill. The central grey carriage is an undifferentiated `M`/`D` block — no downward-pointing tool bit, no clamped workpiece, and crucially **no notch cut into a block** to signal material removal. The two cyan rectangles read as two separate windows rather than ONE glass enclosure wrapping a machine. A player cannot tell this is subtractive milling vs. the additive print tower. This is the criterion that must be unmistakable, and it isn't.
7. **Attractive — BORDERLINE.** Walls and silhouette are appealing, but the busy/speckled roof border and the ambiguous center machine drag it down. Not yet "happy to ship."

## Painter-actionable tweaks
1. **Build ONE glass enclosure, not two windows.** In the roof center (JSON rows ~16–22), make the glass box a single `K`-framed rectangle whose interior is uniform translucent cyan (`c` body with `h` highlight top-left, `g` shade bottom-right). Right now the left panel (`c`/`h`) and right panel (`g`) read as two different skylights — unify them into one box outlined in `K` on all four sides so the machine reads as "inside" it.
2. **Add a clamped workpiece block with a visible cut notch.** Inside the glass box, just under the spindle, place a small `D` block (3–4px wide) with a clear `K`/`d` **notch already carved out of its top** (remove 1–2 pixels from the block's top edge). This is THE subtractive read — material missing from a clamped block. Without the notch the building looks additive/generic.
3. **Make the tool bit point straight down.** Below the grey carriage (rows ~18–20, center), add a 1px-wide, 2–3px-tall stubby `M`/`D` **spindle bit** descending toward the workpiece notch. Currently the carriage just floats as a grey slab with nothing pointing at the part.
4. **Differentiate the spindle carriage from the rails.** The carriage should be a compact `K`-outlined boxy block straddling two thin parallel `D` rails. Right now carriage and rails blur into one grey mass. Outline the carriage in `K` and drop the rails to a single darker `D`/`d` pixel-line each so the carriage clearly rides ON them.
5. **De-speckle the roof border / corrugation (rows ~20–28).** Replace the scattered `L`/`D`/`o` single pixels around the diamond edge with clean, evenly-spaced parallel `D` ribbed lines (corrugated metal) running with the roof slope. The goal is a few crisp ribs, not random dots — kill the mush.
6. **Tighten the coolant mist + swarf so it reads as a cut point.** The cyan+yellow puff under the carriage is on the right track but soft. Concentrate it: a tight 2–3px `c`/`h` mist puff exactly at the bit/workpiece contact, plus 2–3 distinct `o` swarf chips curling up-and-away to one side (not scattered symmetrically). Right now it spreads as a fuzzy band rather than a focused cut.
7. **Make the DRO/readout strip explicit.** Add a short horizontal `g` cyan strip (3–4px) on the FRONT face of the glass box frame (front eave of the box) so it reads as a glowing readout panel, distinct from the glass interior.
