# Review — Research Plan (gateway), round 4

**Reference:** `art_ref/mockups/research_plan.png`
**Render:** `art/preview/research_plan_generic.png`
**Verdict: SHIP**

This iteration finally reads as the *same* building as the drawing: two drafting-desk
units in an L, meeting at a back corner, with an open front notch and a pen holder
fanning up out of it. All the blueprint's must-have features are present and placed
where the drawing puts them.

## Fidelity check (blueprint acceptance criteria)
- **Silhouette / L-arrangement — PASS.** Two distinct units (not one box). Left unit is
  taller and larger; right unit is lower, smaller, and set forward-right. They meet at a
  back corner with an open front notch between them. Matches the drawing's stacking and
  offset.
- **Focal pen holder — PASS (hero is unmistakable).** A cup in the front notch with
  several thin vertical pens/rulers fanning up (orange + teal + white), and an **orange
  V/chevron accent at the base**. This is exactly the hero the blueprint names, and it
  dominates the front notch the way the drawing intends.
- **Left unit, three layers — PASS.** Top slanted drawing-screen (glass scribble) → middle
  teal shelf band → plain lower body box, with an **orange trim post on the left corner**.
  All three stories are legible.
- **Right unit, two layers — PASS.** Smaller slanted screen (teal glass scribble) on top,
  lower body below with a **square window framed by orange trim** on its front face.
- **Base gates — PASS.** True 2:1 iso; roof/top faces visible on both units; front corner
  (the notch) points to the viewer; crisp hard pixels with a clean `K #241f38` outline
  (no blur/AA/mush); the angled screens read as glass; and the sprite is exactly on the
  13-char generic palette (verified: 13 sprite colors + 1 preview-background color, zero
  stray colors).

## Minor polish notes (non-blocking — do NOT gate on these)
- The left unit's **middle shelf band** merges slightly with the top screen's teal; a one-
  pixel darker separation line between the slanted screen and the shelf band would read as
  three cleaner stacked stories, like the drawing's clear middle recess.
- The glass scribbles lean **teal** where the drawing leans **blue/purple**. Teal is in the
  palette (h/c/g/G) so this is acceptable, but a few purple/lavender pixels mixed into the
  screens would nudge the mood closer to the reference.
- The pen-holder cup reads a touch **boxy/visor-like**; slightly narrowing its top would
  make it read more as a cup, but the hero is already recognizable.

These are optional refinements — the render matches the drawing on every graded axis.
