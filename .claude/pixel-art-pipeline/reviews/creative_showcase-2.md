## Spectacular + cohesive with the four creatives
Fully in the family language:

  capped by the star silhouette — same emitter-stream treatment as the other four.

  with `W` signal blips) — a fresh element that stays inside the family's teal/orange
  accent balance and echoes the ops-hub orbital ring.
- `W` sparkles + gold motes, gold cornice + base plinth, tall glowing gold-framed
  teal gallery windows, warm-lit arched entrance, small teal star emblem on the roof.

## Reads as its gateway identity
Unmistakable. The floating **award star** + **broadcasting rings** + two orange
**fair pennants** on the back roof corners say "present at the fair / broadcast your
findings." The pennants are a smart, legible showcase cue that none of the other
four use, so it stays distinct within the set.

## Craft
Crisp iso; unbroken `K` outline around the base; clean drop shadow; windows and
gold trim are sharp. No stray pixels or broken silhouette.

1. The star's shaded lower-right point carries a slightly heavy `G` cluster; trimming
   one row would make the gem read even cleaner, but it currently reads correctly as
   upper-left-lit directional shading.
2. The rooftop beacon pedestal where the beam narrows in is a touch busier than the
   ops-hub emitter ring; a hair more contrast between spire and pedestal could sharpen
   it. Acceptable as is.
3. The band of negative space between the star and the outer gold ring is airy
   (intentional "signal propagation"); one or two more faint `W` blips there could
   enrich it.
