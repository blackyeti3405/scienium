## What's working

## What blocks SHIP (concrete)
1. **Satellite dish floats detached.** The teal dish upper-right has no visible strut/arm connecting it to the roof — it reads as a stray artifact, not part of the building. Anchor it with a clear K-outlined arm to the roof, or reseat it directly on the roofline. Blueprint also wants it to "crackle with `c` signal arcs" — currently static; add 2–3 `c` arc pixels.
2. **Roof/dome is muddy.** The upper mass is a lumpy mid-purple blob — the `dome` doesn't read as a clean curved dome and the silhouette/K-outline up there is jagged. Rebuild the shading with real light-to-dark ramp (W/L highlight on the lit NW face, D/K on the shadow side) and tidy the outline so the dome curve reads. Right now the whole mid-section (roof + telescope base + dish) blurs into one grey zone.
3. **Beam is too thin/faint.** Compared to `terrabase_deep-earth-observatory_t5`'s bold bright column, this beam is a narrow muddy grey/teal stub. Widen it and give it a bright `W`/`c` core so it visibly links the telescope to the hologram — the beam should sell the "firing into the galaxy" story.

## Minor / optional
- The hologram reads more as **atomic/orbital rings** than a galaxy spiral. To sell "galaxy," bias one or two arcs into a spiral arm and sprinkle a few extra h/c star points at varying brightness.
- Particle cloud is a touch sparse vs the `_t5` refs — a few more W/c sparkles trailing off the beam would enrich it.

Fix items 1–3 and this jumps to SHIP.
